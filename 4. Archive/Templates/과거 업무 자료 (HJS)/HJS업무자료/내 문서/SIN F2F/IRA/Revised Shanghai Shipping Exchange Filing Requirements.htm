TO:         IRA PRINCIPALS

FROM:           IRA SECRETARIAT – COMMERCIAL MANAGEMENT LTD.

 

 

As we have advised before, China’s Shanghai Shipping Exchange (SSE) is amending the filing requirements for individual carriers (i.e. this is the filing that you must do yourselves as carriers operating in China, and has nothing to do with the agreement filing we do for IRA).  This new filing requirement goes into effect today.  However, there is a 3-month grace period during which time no penalties will apply.  From 15 Feb 2014, however, lines who are still not in compliance with the new requirements may be penalized.

 

In order to ensure clear understanding, our IRA legal counsel in Beijing has forwarded to us the attached 8 documents from the SSE listing out the requirements:

 

1.     Filing Form For Negotiated Rate (also with English translation from our legal counsel)

2.     Filing Form For Tariff Rate (also with English translation from our legal counsel)

3.     Freight Filing Application Form for International liner Operator

4.     Operational Manual of Accurate Freight Filing

5.     Codes of Base Ports & Base Harbors

6.     HS Customs Codes

7.     Codes of Container Type, Container Size and Cargo Category

8.     Codes of Shipping Routes, Weekly Sailings and Transportation Clause

 

Should members have any comments or queries, the SSE has advised that they are prepared to address those, and are open to the possibility of holding further training sessions, if such need exists among shipping lines.  We suggest, therefore, that you coordinate with your Chinese offices to ensure compliance from your side.
