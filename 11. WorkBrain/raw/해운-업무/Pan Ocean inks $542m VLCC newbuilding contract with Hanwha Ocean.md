Four crude carriers said to have been ordered against employment contract from South Korean oil major SK Energy

![Hanwha Ocean is scheduled to deliver the four VLCCs by February 2030.](https://image.dngroup.com/global/nhst/binary/5a76fd1c4a3672155f793c2571de6dc3?crop=900%2C600%2Cx66%2Cy0%2Csafe&width=980&format=auto&quality=80)

Hanwha Ocean is scheduled to deliver the four VLCCs by February 2030. Photo: Hanwha Ocean

Hanwha Ocean has won a KRW 800.1bn ($524m) contract to build four VLCCs, in one of the year’s larger crude tanker orders at the South Korean yard.

The buyer, which Hanwha identified only as “an Asian shipping company” in a weekend stock exchange filing, is compatriot owner Pan Ocean, according to multiple shipbuilding sources.

The deal works out at about $131m per vessel, with deliveries due by February 2030.

At $131m each, the vessels are priced about $500,000 above the latest VLCC benchmark from Clarksons’ Shipping Intelligence Weekly.

That is still well short of the all-time high of around $150m, reached during the 2008 shipping boom.

Seoul-listed Pan Ocean announced in May that it was ordering the quartet as part of its expansion into the tanker sector.

Although it did not disclose the shipyard, Pan Ocean said the vessels would be delivered by November 2030.

The tankers are backed by long-term charters to South Korean oil major SK Energy, according to shipbuilding sources.

The charters’ duration and rates have not been disclosed.

Shipping sources in Seoul said the four VLCCs were originally to have been ordered by SK Shipping.

Pan Ocean is understood to have stepped in after agreeing to buy 10 of SK Shipping’s existing VLCCs for a reported $676m.

The deal also transferred the long-term charter contracts tied to the 10 secondhand VLCCs to Pan Ocean, including agreements with SK Energy and other South Korean oil majors.

SK Shipping is a subsidiary company of private equity firm Hahn & Co.

In addition to these four vessels, Pan Ocean has another VLCC on order at Qingdao Beihai in China and two VLCCs under construction at HD Hyundai Heavy Industries.

The latter pair was ordered last year at a reported price of $127m each and are scheduled for delivery in the first half of 2028.

Pan Ocean operates a diversified fleet of 115 vessels, including VLOCs, LNG carriers, MR tankers and bulk carriers.
