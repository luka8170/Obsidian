Who we are today

## From one idea to a global group

WR Group Holding GmbH is a privately owned, Germany-headquartered holding company that operates a group of companies operating across more than 70 countries. The Group is structured around four core business areas:

Project logistics

EPC services

Certification and standards compliance

Real estate development and property management

We serve industries where safety, reliability, and technical control are critical. These include energy, petrochemicals, healthcare, and engineering.
