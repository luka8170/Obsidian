# POSSM-BV FIVE YEAR FEE AGREEMENT (2025)

This Five-Year Fee Agreement (the "AGREEMENT") has been made on 2025/06/18

## by and between:

**POS SM Co., LTD.**, a company registered under the laws of Republic of Korea under number _______, whose registered office is at 102, Jungang-daero, Jung-gu, Busan, Republic of Korea (hereinafter referred to as "COMPANY");

**AND**

**BUREAU VERITAS SA BRANCH OFFICE KOREA**, a company registered under the laws of Republic of Korea under number _______, whose registered office is at 24th Floor Samsung Financial Plaza, 639, Jungang-daero, Busanjin-gu, Busan, Korea (hereinafter referred to as "BV");

COMPANY and BV may be hereafter individually referred to as "Party" and jointly as "Parties".

## WHEREAS:

BV is an international classification society member of the International Association of Class Societies ("IACS").

COMPANY is a reputable company engaged in ship trading activities and is soliciting BV services.

The objective of this AGREEMENT is to set the terms and conditions for the Five-Year classification and/or certification of the vessels listed in Annex A.

## IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

### 1. GENERAL

1.1 The vessels managed by COMPANY are classed with and/or certified (e.g. ISM, ISPS, MLC, MRV, DCS or national certification) by BV at the request of their owner in accordance with the Bureau Veritas Marine & Offshore General Conditions and its published rules for classification.

1.2 COMPANY declares to be validly representing the owner of the vessels for the purpose of their classification with and/or certification by BV and warrants the payment of BV fees and expenses in relation thereof.

1.3 By this AGREEMENT, COMPANY requests BV, which accepts, to carry out the periodical surveys of the vessels for the maintenance of their statutory certification and/or classification against an agreed annual amount and a centralized invoicing to the COMPANY. For the avoidance of doubt, the term "survey" shall also be employed in this AGREEMENT to designate audits, inspections or surveys related to other certifications.

1.4 This AGREEMENT concerns only the organization of the periodical surveys mentioned above and their payment. It completes on these points the Bureau Veritas Marine & Offshore General Conditions which form an integral part of this AGREEMENT and are appended to it in Annex D. In case of discrepancy, or doubt as to the interpretation, between Articles 1 to 8 of this AGREEMENT and any clause of Annex D, the latter shall prevail.

This AGREEMENT does not modify the classification conditions signed by the ship owner or on its behalf for the vessel and according to which she is classed and/or certified.

1.5 This AGREEMENT cancels and replaces any fee agreement existing before the date of entry into force of the AGREEMENT.

### 2. SCOPE OF SURVEYS

2.1 BV undertakes to perform the periodical classification and statutory surveys as listed in Annex C for the vessels listed in Annex A of this AGREEMENT in accordance with the requirements set forth in the current classification rules and statutory regulations of the flag administration for which the classification society is acting.

2.2 The surveys listed in Annex C of the AGREEMENT can be modified due to changes in:
- a) BV classification rules;
- b) National and international applicable regulations;
- c) Recognition of Bureau Veritas Marine & Offshore by Flag States;
- d) Vessels classification notations or assignment of new ones, at COMPANY or BV request.

### 3. ORGANIZATION OF SURVEYS

3.1 The ports where the vessels have to be presented for periodical survey are those listed in Annex B to the AGREEMENT which shall be updated on annual basis.

3.2 The surveys are carried out during normal working days and hours in the country where they take place.

3.3 The instalments under this AGREEMENT are inclusive of the fees and the incurred expenses for the surveys carried out under the conditions of above paragraphs 3.1 to 3.2. Travel and reporting time are included.

3.4 However, subject to the applicable national and international regulations, for any surveyor's attendance required outside normal working days and hours in the country where they take place (e.g. attendance by night during the week, attendance on week-ends or public holidays) the overtime in relation to surveys and travel time will be charged separately as per paragraph 4.6 of Article 4 hereafter.

3.5 All surveys are to be properly prepared by COMPANY and arranged with due diligence so as to minimize delays, interferences, travel and waiting time for the surveyor. In case a surveyor is unable to attend the ship due to insufficient preparation or inappropriate arrangements by COMPANY or ship's crew, COMPANY shall compensate any reasonable additional costs thereby incurred by BV.

### 4. FEES

4.1 The fees, excluding taxes, for the services under the AGREEMENT, as described in Articles 1, 2 and 3 here above, are mentioned in Annex A (the Standard Fees). These Standard Fees cover the periodical surveys due over one term of 5 years.

4.2 The Standard Fees (Annex A) will be annually adjusted to take into account:

4.2.1 the inflation rate in the countries where the surveys are supposed to be performed according to Annex B to the AGREEMENT or alternatively, as relevant, the OECD (Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development) net price inflation index;

4.2.2 the age of the ships as per the applicable regulations and BV rules.

4.3 The amount to be paid for each vessel can be modified during the validity of the contract due to changes in the scope of services according to paragraph 2.2 of Article 2 here above.

4.4 If a vessel is removed from the AGREEMENT, the balance of fees is calculated from the commencement of the contract taking into account surveys actually carried out at the effective termination date with consideration of paragraph 7.3 of Article 7 hereafter.

4.5 If a vessel is entered into the AGREEMENT, the fees will be computed from that date of entry until the end of the term of the AGREEMENT and taking into account the updated list of surveys in Annex C.

4.6 Surveys not covered by the Standard Fees - e.g. occasional surveys after damage or other events, surveys arranged to deal with recommendations -, surveys normally covered by the Standard Fees but not reasonably fulfilling the conditions of Article 3, overtime in relation to surveys covered by the AGREEMENT, travel time in relation to surveys performed in ports other than those mentioned in Annex B, extra charges incurred by BV - e.g. local taxes, VAT, certification dues to some Flag States, service suppliers hiring-, will be charged separately according to hourly fees scale in force at the time and place of the survey and the expenses will be charged at cost. Other works carried out remotely are neither covered by the Standard Fees.

### 5. PAYMENT TERMS

5.1 COMPANY agrees that the terms of payment will be as per Annex A with the same periodicity on the following years and until the end of the AGREEMENT.

5.2 COMPANY agrees to pay BV for the services in accordance with clause 6 of the Bureau Veritas Marine & Offshore General Conditions attached in Annex D.

### 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each Party, its employees and agents (on behalf of whom this Article is affirmed) agree to maintain as confidential any information derived from the other Party in connection with this AGREEMENT, and shall not use or disclose to any third party (except as may be required by law, a court of competent jurisdiction or any governmental or regulatory authority or for the purpose of financial audits) without any prior written consent from the other Party.

### 7. VALIDITY AND TERMINATION OF THE AGREEMENT

7.1 Each Party agrees that the terms of this AGREEMENT shall become effective from the date of signing on this AGREEMENT from both Parties and remain in full force and effect for five (5) years from the effective date, with tacit re-conduction after each period of five (5) years, unless terminated before that date in accordance with paragraph 7.2 hereof.

7.2 Either Party reserves the right to terminate this AGREEMENT if any of the following events should occur:

7.2.1 If any vessel covered by this AGREEMENT is no longer managed by COMPANY;

7.2.2 If either Party gives the other Party thirty (30) days' written advance notice of termination at any time;

7.2.3 If COMPANY fails to make payment to BV when due under this AGREEMENT.

7.3 In case a survey is being performed or started during the termination notice period, the fees covering the survey will be included in the balance referred to in paragraph 4.4 of Article 4 above.

7.4 In the event of termination of this AGREEMENT, all due payments which are not yet settled at the time of termination date shall be paid to BV within thirty (30) days.

### 8. SPECIAL CONDITIONS

8.1 The following special conditions (hereinafter the "Special Conditions") are agreed between the Parties and shall be applicable to the present Agreement. The present Special Conditions are meant to be used jointly with the BUREAU VERITAS MARINE & OFFSHORE GENERAL CONDITIONS and shall supersede the relating parts of BUREAU VERITAS MARINE & OFFSHORE GENERAL CONDITIONS (hereinafter the "General Conditions") as further detailed.

8.2 The Special Conditions shall modify the General Conditions as follow:

8.2.1 The article 15 shall be deleted and replaced by the following:

15.1 These Conditions shall be construed in accordance with and governed by the laws of England and Wales.

15.2 Any dispute shall be finally settled under the London Maritime Arbitrators Association (LMAA) Terms, which are deemed to be incorporated by reference into this clause. The arbitration shall be conducted in London (United Kingdom), and the number of arbitrators shall be three (3), unless the parties agree otherwise. The Parties agree to keep the arbitration proceedings confidential.

15.3 Notwithstanding clause 15.2, disputes relating to the payment of the Society's invoices may, at the Society's sole discretion, be submitted to the competent courts of England and Wales, or to any other competent local court.

8.3 Except as amended hereby by these Special Conditions, the articles of the General Conditions shall remain in full force and effect.

8.4 All capitalized and upper-case terms used herein shall have the same meaning as ascribed to them in the General Conditions, unless otherwise provided in the Special Conditions.

8.5 In the event of any discrepancy, ambiguity or inconsistency between the content of the Special Conditions and any provision of the General Conditions in relation to the matters covered herein, the provisions of the Special Conditions shall prevail to the extent of such conflicting provisions of the General Conditions.
