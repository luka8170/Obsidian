# Namsung Group - January 2026 Updates

## 2026-01-02 - Feeder Container Newbuilding Order

### Summary
남성그룹(남성해운, 동영해운)이 중국 CSSC 산하 황푸웬총조선소에 피더 컨테이너선 4척을 발주했다. 한국과 일본 조선소에서만 선박을 건조해온 남성그룹이 처음으로 중국 조선소를 선택한 것으로, 가격 경쟁력 확보와 선대 강화를 목표로 한다.

### Order Details

#### Namsung Shipping (남성해운)
- **Vessel Type**: 4,300 TEU Container Ship
- **Quantity**: 2 vessels
- **Shipyard**: Huangpu Wenchong Shipbuilding (CSSC Group, China)
- **Contract Price**: USD 60.5M per vessel
- **Total Value**: USD 121M
- **Delivery Schedule**:
  - Vessel #1: June 2028
  - Vessel #2: October 2028
- **Purpose**: India East Coast → West Coast route expansion

#### Dong Young Shipping (동영해운)
- **Vessel Type**: 1,900 TEU Container Ship
- **Quantity**: 2 vessels
- **Shipyard**: Huangpu Wenchong Shipbuilding (CSSC Group, China)
- **Contract Price**: USD 32M per vessel
- **Total Value**: USD 64M
- **Delivery Schedule**:
  - Vessel #1: October 2028
  - Vessel #2: December 2028
- **Purpose**: Vietnam (Haiphong) & Southeast Asia route strengthening

### Total Investment
- **Total Vessels**: 4 ships
- **Total Value**: USD 185M
- **Average Price**: USD 46.25M per vessel
