# POSSM CEO Visit — Meeting Minutes

**Date:** Thursday, 5 March 2026  
**Venue:** POSSM Head Office (Busan)  
**Attendees:**  Dong-Gyun Kim (CEO, POSSM), Tae-Il Park (Technical Director, POSSM), Christophe Vaes(BV Korea Director), Sang-Jin Han 

---

## Purpose of Visit

The meeting was intended to express appreciation for the existing BV–POSSM partnership and to explore avenues for deeper cooperation going forward.

---

## Meeting Notes

POSSM CEO Dong-Gyun Kim has hands-on experience in West Africa (Nigeria, Angola, etc.), which made for a natural and relaxed opening to the conversation.

**POSSM Current Status**  
POSSM currently has one vessel classed with BV, which is reported to be operating without issue. The company is exploring a strategic shift from its core dry bulk business toward wet segment (tankers), looking to diversify its portfolio. Given its strong position as a leading Korean dry bulk operator, the move into wet appears to be the logical next step in their growth strategy.

**LNG and Alternative Fuel Transition**  
POSSM expressed high satisfaction with BV's technical support during the construction of their LNG carrier built at Hanwha Ocean.

We introduced BV's Future Ship Team (FST), based in Singapore and Shanghai, which has extensive experience in alternative fuel transition simulations and roadmap development — covering LNG, methanol, and ammonia. A Dutch expert from the FST who worked on the Carnival cruise fleet fuel transition project is scheduled to visit Korea soon.

POSSM acknowledged that "changing the fleet is no longer optional," reflecting their awareness of international regulatory pressure. They expressed genuine interest in collaborating with BV FST on a fuel transition roadmap. Technical Director Tae-Il Park was identified as the key decision-maker on this topic.

**Vale Tender**  
The Vale iron ore carrier tender is currently ongoing. Korean shipowners understood to be participating include Pan Ocean (POSSM), HMM, and GLOVIS. (H-Line has officially announced it will not participate.) Vale appears to be focusing on its existing Korean partners.

CEO Kim confirmed directly that "we are one of them" — POSSM is participating in the Vale tender. The vessel type is understood to be a Newcastlemax with three alternative fuel options. Pan Ocean has approximately 15 years of history with Vale, with the first vessel delivered back in 2011.

*(Note: GLOVIS has already committed to requesting a BV proposal should they be awarded the Vale contract.)*

**Woodside LNGC**  
GLOVIS was mentioned as the likely Korean partner for the Woodside LNGC project, possibly the only Korean shipowner involved. CEO Kim confirmed that Pan Ocean is not participating.

**New Rongcheng Shipyard (China)**  
This previously bankrupt shipyard holds four drydocks. Block fabrication has partially resumed (for Wuhu Shipyard), but no independent newbuilding projects have been launched yet. It was mentioned as a potential candidate shipyard for the Vale project. CEO Kim indicated he would seek additional information from Christophe before any final decision — a sign of confidence in BV's shipyard intelligence network in China.

**Middle East Situation**  
Christophe raised the potential impact of the ongoing Middle East conflict on POSSM's fleet operations. CEO Kim responded that there has been no direct impact so far in terms of food or fuel supply, though prolonged instability would be a concern. He noted that POSSM has roughly 60 years of history operating in the Iraq–Iran region and has weathered three wars over that period.
