## Strategic Implications

- **Bureau Veritas Success**: Exceptional growth in LNG carrier market through BV+KR strategy
- **Market Specialization**: 2022 shows strong focus on high-value LNG carriers vs. diverse 2021 portfolio
- **Dual Certification Trend**: BV+KR combination proves successful in premium market segments
- **Competitive Positioning**: BV established as major player in Korean newbuilding market
- **Future Opportunities**: Build on LNG success while exploring other premium segments

## Market Outlook

The 2022 results demonstrate a dramatic transformation in both market composition and Bureau Veritas' position. The shift toward LNG carriers and dual certification has created opportunities for international classification societies to compete more effectively with Korean Register. Bureau Veritas' successful partnership strategy with KR in the LNG segment provides a strong foundation for continued growth in the premium shipbuilding market.
