**Subject:** Approval request – USD 6,500/vessel/year FYF for Hana Marine fleet

---

## Ask

We request approval of a **USD 6,500 per vessel per year** Fixed Yearly Fee (FYF) for all 8 Hana Marine vessels.

## Background

- **Client complaint:** Hana Marine's CEO called us directly, saying BV's survey fees run 2–3x higher than competitor KR. He also asked that a recent occasional (underwater) survey charge be revisited — a BV-certified diver cost KRW 5,000,000 vs. KRW 1,200,000 for a standard diver.

- **Newbuilding opportunity:** Hana Marine plans to consolidate all future newbuildings with a single Japanese shipyard. Closing the FYF discussion now gives us a real chance to secure class on those newbuildings, but the shipyard currently leans toward NK, and even Hana Marine's own Busan staff are recommending KR.

- **Latest update from Japan (BV Japan):** The Kegoya Dockyard newbuilding was expected to be contracted in July–August. This week, **Shin Kurushima Dockyard visited BV Kobe** and discussed a 4,400 DWT chemical tanker for Hana Marine — a positive meeting, with a real chance BV is accepted as class. However, in that same meeting Hana Marine again said our FYF level is "much higher than they are asking." In short, the newbuilding opportunity is widening, but every conversation keeps coming back to the existing fleet's FYF price.

## Financial Justification

At USD 6,500/vessel/year, per the simulation in the `1. 하나마린 FYF 체결` folder (latest update, 5-year cumulative, USD):

- Fleet-wide margin **27.3%**, every vessel above 23% — no vessel goes into loss.
- For the 3 largest vessels (WOORI, GOLDEN GRACE, ACE HANA), current actual billing already exceeds USD 6,500 — a real price cut for the client, while margin stays healthy for BV.
- For the 2 smaller vessels (LUNA, MIR HANA), USD 6,500 is a modest increase over current actuals, but balances the fleet and gives the client one predictable price.

## Points to Note

- Because the fee is flat, larger vessels earn a lower margin (~23.5%) than smaller vessels (~29%) — still profitable, but worth revisiting at next renewal.
- The longer this decision takes, the higher the risk Hana Marine moves to NK or KR.

## Requested Next Steps

1. Approve USD 6,500/vessel/year FYF.
2. Confirm our intent to pursue class on the newbuildings, so we can communicate this clearly to the client.
