## Strategic Implications & Required Adjustments

### Understanding the Target Philosophy

The reduced GRT targets indicate HQ expects Korea to:

1. **Focus on Achievable Wins**
   - Smaller, more certain projects rather than chasing mega-deals
   - Build track record before pursuing larger vessels
   - Quality over quantity in initial market penetration

2. **Segment-Specific Strategy**
   - **NC Owner (16,500 avg)**: Small-to-medium chemical tankers, CTVs, ferries, general cargo, coastal vessels
   - **NC Yard (100,000 avg)**: Continue yard relationships for larger projects
   - **CAC (30,000 avg)**: Medium-sized transfer opportunities

3. **Realistic Market Share**
   - Accept Korea's 11.56% allocation within NAZ is appropriate given current position
   - Build foundation before expecting larger allocation

### Critical Market Reality

**Challenge**: Korean shipowners currently ordering vessels averaging 40,000-70,000 GRT
- HMM: 13K TEU containerships (~100,000+ GRT each)
- SK Shipping: 100K VLEC (~90,000 GRT each)
- Hyundai Glovis: LNG Carriers (~100,000+ GRT each)

**My Approach**: 
- **Primary focus**: Identify and secure 6-8 smaller vessel opportunities (10,000-25,000 GRT)
  - Chemical tanker segment (KT Marine, Sungho Shipping type projects)
  - Ferry/RoPax operators
  - Coastal shipping companies
  - Specialized vessels (CTVs, service vessels)
  
- **Secondary pursuit**: Continue relationship building with major owners for 2027+ pipeline
  - These won't count toward 2026 unless vessel size fits
  - Build foundation for future larger allocations
