Dear SangJin,

Thank you for sharing information about Hyundai Glovis.

Some words about WTIV:

Presently, there are ~100 WTIV in the global fleet, where ¾ is dedicated to the Chinese market (PRC) and classed by CCS (72 units). BV has only 2 units in the Clarksons’ list, compared to 11 by ABS and 16 by DNV. However, we may count 7 ships, even 9 if we include two SOV with significant lifting capacity (cf. tables attached).

WTIV ordering remains limited due to turbine upsizing concerns, an oversupplied Chinese market and constrained shipyard capacity. Only one WTIV order has been placed globally in the past year.

The European WTIV supply/demand balance appears set to remain tight over the coming years (peak utilisation of >90% is projected in 2027/28), supported by increasing installation activity. Some charterers have booked units several years in advance and for longer durations (contract lead times are currently averaging ~2 years). Day rates for high spec WTIVs have increased by a very strong 56% since start-2023.

There are two documents available for clients (here attached) regarding offshore wind vessels but not specifically WTIV. There is also a ppt available at Sharepoint (let me know, I would transfer it by Teams due to the size).

Our strong point is our position in 3 major companies engaged in offshore works: Jan de Nul, Deme, and Boskalis.

Do you think that a specific presentation about WTIV would be needed? We could refer to the 3 main vessels: 42398M “ZHAO SHANG CHENG FENG”, 37163W “VOLTAIRE”, and 38244W “LES ALIZÉS”.

Regarding my availability to attend a meeting, it can be next week (after 10 am CET on Wednesday) and I am flexible in the following weeks until Christmas. However, I would need a little time beforehand if a presentation would be requested.

Dear Mr. Guerrero,

As we are trying to secure the LCO2 vessel (Charterer: SK E&S, Ship Operator: SK Shipping), we previously conducted a webinar with SK E&S. 

They were pleased with the webinar but felt it lacked sufficient coverage of the operational aspects. 

They are particularly concerned about the low-pressure system and want a deeper understanding of the LCO2 vessel, including issues related to loading and unloading.

Therefore, it would be beneficial to organize another webinar that includes both SK E&S and SK Shipping. 

SK Shipping has already participated in webinars and studies with our competitors and has a considerable amount of knowledge. 

However, if we focus on operational details and material aspects, it will demonstrate BV's comprehensive understanding of LCO2 vessels.

I believe that these LCO2 vessels represent a good starting point and could lead to more opportunities in the future.
