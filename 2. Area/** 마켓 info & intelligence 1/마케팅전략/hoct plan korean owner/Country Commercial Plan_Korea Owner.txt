PURPOSE OF THE DOCUMENT | GUIDANCE FOR THE TEAM

This template is designed to guide the development of country-level commercial plans for Bureau Veritas Marine & 
Offshore. The purpose of this document is to:
1.Provide a structured framework for market and competitive analysis
2.Guide the development of clear, actionable sales and marketing strategies
3.Ensure alignment between local initiatives and global commercial priorities
4.Facilitate effective communication of plans to senior leadership



When completed, this plan will serve as a comprehensive roadmap for commercial success in each territory. It will 
function as a strategic planning tool and ultimately implemented in our commercial & performance tools namely 
Salesforce and Vivagoals.
The completed plan should be:
.Data-driven and insightful
.Specific to each market's unique challenges and opportunities
.Focused on achievable objectives with clear metrics for success under the format of OKRs



Thoughtful completion of each section is crucial in shaping our collective path forward. This document is not just a 
report, but a dynamic tool to guide commercial efforts throughout the year and should be updated every 6 months.
The final plan will be reviewed by senior management to support and enable local commercial success and set sales 
objectives of each year.
