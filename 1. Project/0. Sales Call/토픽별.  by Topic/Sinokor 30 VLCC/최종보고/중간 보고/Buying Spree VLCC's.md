**주제:** Re: Sinokor Merchant : Buying Spree VLCC's  
  

Dear Mr. Arora,

I hope you had a great start to the New Year.

  

As you remember, Sinokor is continuing to buy VLCCs this year after purchasing 8 vessels last year.

Last year, Sinokor told us they wanted to class their new VLCCs with BV. They said if we could adjust FYF a bit—offering a yearly fee of $17,000/year for VLCCs over 10 years old and $14,000/year for younger ones—they would arrange CAC to BV for  all 8 vessels . However, our management decided we couldn't lower the rates any further. As a result, all 8 VLCCs went to KR, who offered better pricing than BV.

  

Today, I talked with the Sinokor team leader who handles classification contracts. About their plan to buy 30 VLCCs, he didn't deny it (which is a positive sign) and said the same thing as last year: "We're not ready to make a formal request yet and will contact you later, but if BV shows some flexibility on pricing, we'll seriously consider classing with BV."

So it looks like the key to winning CAC for these 30 VLCCs will be whether we can slightly adjust our current pricing with Sinokor, despite the recent low profitability on our existing VLCC contracts.

  

I'm available on January 19th, 20th, or 21st. Please let me know which date and time work for you, and I'll join the call.

Looking forward to hearing from you.
