Here is the first monthly analysis of the Korean fleet, based on the Clarkson data from the Commercial team. The workbook is built as pivot-style views. Each month you only replace the source tab and every view updates.

**What it covers (as you asked)**

1. **Fleet age by owner and segment** — the whole Korean-owned fleet, not only BV-classed ships.
2. **Class split by segment** — BV share vs. other societies, for ships in service, shown by number of ships and by GT. Each row adds up to 100% (a dual-class ship is split equally between its societies).
3. **In-service focus** — the orderbook is in a separate tab; all share figures use in-service ships only.

**Main points (September 2026, in service)**

- **BV is strong in only a few segments.** Crude Tankers about 45% (49% by GT) and LNG about 15%. Very low in Bulkers, Containers, Car Carriers, Product/Chemical and LPG (0–6%).

Clarkson does not show the class of orderbook ships until delivery, so recent deliveries are the only reliable read.

- **Recent deliveries (2023–2026) show today's preference:** KR 60–74%, BV 1–12%. The only real challenger on new ships is **ABS, and this is almost all Cido Shipping**. Cido has taken about 18 ABS ships since 2023 (containers, bulkers, LPG, crude, product) and has 63 more on order: 20 car carriers, 20 product/chemical tankers, 13 crude tankers, 10 bulkers. ABS's share will keep rising as these deliver. 

- **Ageing fleet:** 618 in-service commercial ships are 20 years or older, mostly in General Cargo, Product/Chemical and Bulk, and mostly with small and mid-size owners. The workbook ranks owners by the number of ships aged 20+, 25+ and 30+ and by ageing ratio.

- **Who actually orders newbuildings:** 271 ships on order, most contracts signed in 2024–2026. Ordering follows owner strategy and size, not fleet age. A small group of frequent builders — **Cido, HMM, Pan Ocean** — places most of the orders and keeps ordering even though their fleets are already young. Most owners with old fleets order little or nothing. So the frequent orderers may deserve as much attention as the old-fleet owners: that is where newbuilding decisions are really made.

- **Room to grow with current customers:** several of our customers still run large non-BV fleets in service — for example Pan Ocean, H-Line.
