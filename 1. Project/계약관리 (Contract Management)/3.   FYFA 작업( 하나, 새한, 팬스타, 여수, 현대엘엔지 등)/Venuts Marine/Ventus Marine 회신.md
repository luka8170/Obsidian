Hi Ai Nah & BH,

I have reviewed the **BV proposal and draft agreement for the BV Five-Year Block Fee**. Below is a summary of my findings and a formal recommendation:

**1. Fee Analysis (Ventus Yulan)**

- **Annual Block Fee:** **USD 4,500 per vessel** (Approx. KRW 6,525,000 at 1,450 KRW/USD).
- **Cost Rationale:** This rate is comparable to the KRW 6,552,609 invoiced for the 2025 Annual Survey. However, the Block Fee covers the Intermediate Survey (2026) and the Special Survey (2028), resulting in savings and budget stability.
- **Inflation Adjustment**: Fees are subject to annual adjustment every January 1st (Clause 4.1).
- **Extra Charges**: Travel & living expenses, overtime, and urgent visit fees will be invoiced separately.

**2. Recommendation: Change of Contracting Party to NMK** I recommend switching the contracting party from 'Ventus Marine Korea' to Njord Marine Korea (NMK) for the following reasons:

- **Regulatory Alignment:** NMK is the official manager holding the **Interim DOC**. This ensures consistency during BV audits and safety management (ISM/SMS).
- **Verified Payment Flow:** I have confirmed that **actual payments can be settled directly from the Vessel Owners' accounts** without any administrative issues, even if the contract is under NMK.
- **Management Efficiency:** Consolidating fleet certification under NMK streamlines both operational and financial oversight.

I have attached the proposal agreement and review report for your reference. I would appreciate your approval to proceed with the contract under Njord Marine Korea.
