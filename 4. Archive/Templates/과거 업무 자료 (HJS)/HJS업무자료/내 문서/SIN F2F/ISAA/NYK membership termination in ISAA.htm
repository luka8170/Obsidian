Email dated 28 November 2013

 

TO:                     ISAA PRINCIPALS

FROM:                ROBERTO GIANNETTA – ISAA SECRETARY

 

 

Please be advised that we have received the attached notice of resignation from NYK.  NYK Singapore has explained to me that NYK Group review their status in all discussion forums periodically.  In their latest review, the decision was unfortunately made to terminate their membership status in this forum.

 

As such, NYK will cease to be members of ISAA with effect from 27 February 2014.  Until this date, NYK are still considered fully-fledged members of ISAA – entitled to participate in statistical data exchange, attend meetings, etc.  Indeed, they will still be attending the upcoming meeting in Singapore next week.  After 27 Feb, however, ISAA and ISAA members at all levels (Principals and local) must cease communication with NYK on any matters relating to this forum.

 

Please be guided accordingly.  We will process the change in our Basic Agreement and with filing authorities.

 

 

 

 

Sincerely,

 

Roberto Giannetta

Secretary for ISAA
