Understanding Doosan Enerbility’s Unique Logistics Requirements

Power Generation Equipment Characteristics

• Gas Turbines: 200-400 tons, extreme precision alignment requirements

• Steam Turbines: 150-300 tons, temperature-sensitive components

• Industrial Boilers: 100-500 tons, complex multi-piece assemblies

• Generators: 100-250 tons, electromagnetic sensitivity concerns

• Heat Recovery Steam Generators (HRSG): Modular systems requiring sequential delivery

Critical Logistics Challenges We Address

1. Precision Tolerance: Equipment alignment within ±2mm specifications

2. Vibration Control: Sensitive turbine components requiring specialized cushioning

3. Sequential Delivery: Just-in-sequence delivery for complex assembly processes

4. Installation Support: Coordination with heary-lift Cranes and installation teams

5. European Market Access: Power plant project logistics across diverse regulatory environments
