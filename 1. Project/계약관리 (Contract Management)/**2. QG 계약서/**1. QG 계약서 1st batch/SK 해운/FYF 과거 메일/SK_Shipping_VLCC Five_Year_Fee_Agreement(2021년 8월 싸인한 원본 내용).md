## IT IS AGREED AS FOLLOWS:

### 1. GENERAL

1.1 The vessels managed by COMPANY are classed with and/or certified (e.g. ISM, ISPS, MLC, MRV, DCS or national certification) by BV at the request of their owner in accordance with the Bureau Veritas Marine & Offshore General Conditions and its published rules for classification.

1.2 COMPANY declares to be validly representing the owner of the vessels for the purpose of their classification with and/or certification by BV and warrants the payment of BV fees and expenses in relation thereof.

1.3 By this AGREEMENT, COMPANY requests BV, which accepts, to carry out the periodical surveys of the vessels for the maintenance of their statutory certification and/or classification against an agreed annual amount and a centralized invoicing to the COMPANY. For the avoidance of doubt, the term "survey" shall also be employed in this AGREEMENT to designate audits, inspections or surveys related to other certifications.

1.4 This AGREEMENT concerns only the organization of the periodical surveys mentioned above and their payment. It completes on these points the Bureau Veritas Marine & Offshore General Conditions which form an integral part of this AGREEMENT and are appended to it in Annex D. In case of discrepancy, or doubt as to the interpretation, between Articles 1 to 8 of this AGREEMENT and any clause of Annex D, the latter shall prevail. For the avoidance of doubt the article 9 of this AGREEMENT shall prevail on the Article 15 of the Annex D.

This AGREEMENT does not modify the classification conditions signed by the ship owner or on its behalf for the vessel and according to which she is classed and/or certified.

1.5 This AGREEMENT cancels and replaces any fee agreement existing before the date of entry into force of the AGREEMENT.

### 2. SCOPE OF SURVEYS

2.1 BV undertakes to perform the periodical classification and statutory surveys as listed in Annex C for the vessels listed in Annex A of this AGREEMENT in accordance with the requirements set forth in the current classification rules and statutory regulations of the flag administration for which the classification society is acting.

2.2 The surveys listed in Annex C of the AGREEMENT can be modified due to changes in:
- a) BV classification rules;
- b) National and international applicable regulations;
- c) Recognition of Bureau Veritas Marine & Offshore by Flag States;
- d) Vessels classification notations or assignment of new ones, at COMPANY or BV request.

### 3. ORGANIZATION OF SURVEYS

3.1 The ports where the vessels have to be presented for periodical survey are those listed in Annex B to the AGREEMENT which may be updated on annual basis.

3.2 The surveys are carried out during normal working days and hours in the country where they take place.

3.3 The instalments under this AGREEMENT are inclusive of the fees for the surveys carried out under the conditions of above paragraphs 3.1 to 3.2. Travel and reporting time are included.

3.4 However, subject to the applicable national and international regulations, for any surveyor's attendance required outside normal working days and hours in the country where they take place (e.g. attendance by night during the week, attendance on week-ends or public holidays) the overtime in relation to surveys and travel time will be charged separately as per paragraph 4.6 of Article 4 hereafter.

3.5 All surveys are to be properly prepared by COMPANY and arranged with due diligence so as to minimize delays, interferences, travel and waiting time for the surveyor. In case a surveyor is unable to attend the ship due to insufficient preparation or inappropriate arrangements by COMPANY or ship's crew, COMPANY shall compensate any reasonable additional costs thereby incurred by BV.

### 4. FEES

4.1 The fees, excluding taxes, for the services under the AGREEMENT, as described in Articles 1, 2 and 3 here above, are mentioned in Annex A (the Standard Fees). These Standard Fees cover the periodical surveys due over one term of 5 years.

4.3 The amount to be paid for each vessel can be modified during the validity of the contract due to changes in the scope of services according to paragraph 2.2 of Article 2 here above.

4.4 If a vessel is removed from the AGREEMENT, the fees of remaining vessels will be computed according to ANNEX F paragraph 2.4 from that date of removal until the end of the term of the AGREEMENT and taking into account the updated list of surveys in Annex C.

4.5 If a vessel is entered into the AGREEMENT, the fees will be computed according to ANNEX F paragraph 2.4 from that date of entry until the end of the term of the AGREEMENT and taking into account the updated list of surveys in Annex C.

4.6 Surveys not covered by the Standard Fees - e.g. occasional surveys after damage or other events, surveys arranged to deal with recommendations -, surveys normally covered by the Standard Fees but not reasonably fulfilling the conditions of Article 3, overtime in relation to surveys covered by the AGREEMENT, extra charges incurred by BV - e.g. local taxes, VAT, certification dues to some Flag States, service suppliers hiring -, will be charged separately according to hourly fees scale in force at the time and place of the survey, and the expenses will be charged at cost. Other works carried out remotely are neither covered by the Standard Fees.

### 5. PAYMENT TERMS

5.1 COMPANY agrees that the terms of payment will be as per Annex A with the same periodicity on the following years and until the end of the AGREEMENT.

5.2 COMPANY agrees to pay BV for the services in accordance with clause 6 of the Bureau Veritas Marine & Offshore General Conditions attached in Annex D.

### 6. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each Party, its employees and agents (on behalf of whom this Article is affirmed) agree to maintain as confidential any information derived from the other Party in connection with this AGREEMENT, and shall not use or disclose to any third party (except as may be required by law, a court of competent jurisdiction or any governmental or regulatory authority or for the purpose of financial audits) without any prior written consent from the other Party.

### 7. VALIDITY AND TERMINATION OF THE AGREEMENT

7.1 Each Party agrees that the terms of this AGREEMENT shall become effective from the date of signing on this AGREEMENT from both Parties and remain in full force and effect until the termination of the contract under the agreement of both parties, unless terminated before that date in accordance with paragraph 7.2 hereof.

7.2 Either Party reserves the right to terminate this AGREEMENT if any of the following events should occur:

7.2.1 If any vessel covered by this AGREEMENT is no longer managed by COMPANY;

7.2.2 If either Party gives the other Party thirty (30) days' written advance notice of termination at any time;

7.2.3 If COMPANY fails to make payment to BV when due under this AGREEMENT.

7.3 In the event of termination of this AGREEMENT, all due payments which are not yet settled at the time of termination date shall be payed to BV within thirty (30) days.

### 8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES

8.1 The Present AGREEMENT shall be construed in accordance with and governed by the laws of England and Wales.

8.2 All disputes arising out of or in connection with the present AGREEMENT shall be finally settled by arbitration under the Rules of the London Court of International Arbitration ("LCIA"). The arbitration shall take place in London. The language of dispute and arbitration shall be the English language. The arbitration decision shall be final and binding on the Parties.

## ANNEX F
### ADDITIONAL COMMERCIAL TERMS

The following Additional Commercial Terms apply to the present AGREEMENT entered between SK SHIPPING CO. LTD ("Client") and BUREAU VERITAS MARINE & OFFSHORE SAS BRANCH OFFICE KOREA ("BV").

### 1. OBJECTIVES OF THE ADDITIONAL TERMS

Objectives of the present Additional Terms are to:
- Agree on additional commercial conditions under which BV shall offer its services and shall complete AGREEMENT in place between the Parties;

### 2. ADDITIONAL COMMERCIAL TERMS

The Parties shall use their best efforts in order to reach the Purpose of the Agreement.

#### 2.1 BV's commitments:

BV will offer to the COMPANY special rates for annual classification fees (hereinafter referred to as the "Class Fee Rates") as defined in the article 2.4. These Class Fee Rates shall be applicable upon COMPANY's completion of its Commitments. The Class Fee Rates offered by BV to the COMPANY shall be applicable for the whole duration of the FYF Agreement. During the term of this contract, the Class Fee Rates shall not be changed until the contract is terminated unless there is an additional written agreement between the parties.

BV shall only charge COMPANY inspection overtime during the execution of the FYF Agreement. The applicable rate for such overtime shall be:
- For overtime in Korea during working days: USD 95 per hour
- For overtime in Korea during weekends and holidays: USD 190 per hour
- For overtime outside of Korea during working days: USD 140 per hour
- For overtime outside of Korea during weekends and holidays: USD 280 per hour

BV shall not charge the COMPANY for:
- Travel expenses during the execution of the FYF Agreement
- Transfer of Class during the execution of the FYF Agreement
- Emergency Response Service during the execution of the FYF Agreement

#### 2.2. COMPANY's commitments:

The COMPANY shall:
- Sign and apply the FYF AGREEMENT
- Fulfil the conditions described in the Annex A to beneficiate from the Class Fee Rates
- Transfer the class of two (2) vessel to BV Class. These vessel are:
  - V.ADVANCE
  - V.GLORY

#### 2.3 Execution of Classification Contracts:

For the avoidance of doubt the present ANNEX shall only be interpreted as an agreement commercial terms with regards to the prices offered by BV to the COMPANY. In case of any discussion related to any other subject relating to the execution of classification services the FYF Agreement and the Bureau Veritas Marine & Offshore General Conditions shall be applied.

#### 2.4 Class Fee Rates

The following Five Year Block Fee shall be applied for any type of ship:

1. **In case the total number of vessel classed by BV is equal or inferior to eight (8) ships:**
   - USD 70,000 per vessel per 5 year

2. **In case the total number of vessel of the COMPANY classed by BV is superior to eight (8):**
   - In case of 9 vessels: USD 65,000 per vessel per 5 year
   - In case of 10 or more vessels: USD 60,000 per vessel per 5 year

3. **In case the total number of vessel of the COMPANY classed by BV is less than eight (8):**
   - In case of 7 vessels: USD 75,000 per vessel per 5 year
   - In case of 6 vessels: USD 80,000 per vessel per 5 year
   - In case of 5 vessels: USD 85,000 per vessel per 5 year
   - In case of 4 vessels: USD 90,000 per vessel per 5 year

#### 2.5 Payment of each Survey

| Item | Annual Survey | Intermediate Survey | Special Survey |
|------|---------------|---------------------|----------------|
| Survey fee | 10% of 5 Year Block Fee | 30% of Year Block Fee | 40% of Year Block Fee |
