Dear Colleagues,

Please be informed that MyPerformance@BV and MyDevelopment@BV Process for Year 2025-2026 will be launched from 24th November 2025.

As you are aware well, it is crucial process of Performance Evaluation and Objective Setting for the upcoming year, 2025-2026.

This process is fundamental in **recognizing and appraising your achievements over the past year** and **establishing clear objectives for the year ahead**.

Your dedication and hard work contributed significantly to the success of our team and the company as a whole.

Please expect further communication providing details below according to the timeline, procedures, and guidelines for the Performance Evaluation and Objective Setting process with your N+1.

Your active participation and commitment to this process are integral to our collective success.

1. **MyPerformance Evaluation Scope**

- **2025 Objective Performance and BV Values**

- **Rating Scale**

1.        Objectives Performance: Exceeds Objectives / Meets Objectives / Below Objectives (self – assessment & N+1 assessment)

**2.**        Values

3.        Overall Performance Rating : Exceeds Expectations / Meets Expectations / Needs Improvements (N+1 assessment)

*** Leadership Expectation section’ has been replaced with the “Coming Soon - Leadership Framework”. (Only Available in the Manager form) . Description has also been changed.**

2. **2025-2026 MyPerformance and MyDevelopment Process will be conducted through SF**

l  MyPerformance Form is created in SF for the concerned employees by HO SF Central HRIS Team **on 24th November 2025**.  (If you did not input your 2025 Performance Objectives, the form will be shown as empty)

n  **Manager Form** : Band IV & Above and/or Employee who has subordinate (Objective Performance, BV Value and Leadership Framework)

n  **Employee Form** : All other employees (Objective Performance and BV Values)

n  2025 Objective plan : **Real-time synchronization with 2025 MyPerformance @BV Form during Self-Assessment session, any update of 2025 objectives will be visible** and reflected in the MyPerformance Form even though after launching.

The revision/update of 2025 objective is to be done after discussion/confirmation of N+1 (Notification is triggered to N+1 once the objectives are updated by an employee)

3. **Eligibility**

l  **2025 Performance Assessment** : applied to all employees who joined **before** **1st October 2025 (If you could not set up 2025 Performance Objectives yet, please input in SF until 21st November 2025)**

l  **2026 Objective Setting** : All employees
