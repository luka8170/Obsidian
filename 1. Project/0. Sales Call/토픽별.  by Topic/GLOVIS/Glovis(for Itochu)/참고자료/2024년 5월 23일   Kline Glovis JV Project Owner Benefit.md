We sincerely appreciate your consideration of BV for your classification needs.

This regulation is uniformly applicable across all other classification societies. Therefore, the HSE Manager from BVS will not be able to participate in your PMC activities.

Given these international regulations, BV is committed to providing you with the utmost benefit through the following proposal:

**1.**       **BV’s Commitment to Supporting HSE Activities During Construction**

**2.**       **Proposed HSE Support and Compliance Plan without any extra cost**

Under the situation that we have no detailed information of your HSE inspection and our survey fee can’t cover the cost of international qualified HSE surveyor, we would like to propose that our HSSE coordinators and HSSE Manager in our site team will assist your HSE manager in your site team to comply with the requirement from the charterer and they will proceed HSE support in accordance with BV’s utmost HSE standard without any extra cost.

**3.**       **QHSE & Operation Director's Regular Site Visits and HSE Training/Inspections without any extra cost**

In addition to above, our QHSE & Operation Director to cover North Asia Zone will visit the site regularly and offer HSE trainings and inspections as required by the owner without any extra cost.

We would like to take this opportunity to emphasize that BV, with its strengths in energy supply chain management compared to other classification societies, is an indispensable business partner for your company.

Thank you for considering our proposal. We look forward to the opportunity to work closely with you.
