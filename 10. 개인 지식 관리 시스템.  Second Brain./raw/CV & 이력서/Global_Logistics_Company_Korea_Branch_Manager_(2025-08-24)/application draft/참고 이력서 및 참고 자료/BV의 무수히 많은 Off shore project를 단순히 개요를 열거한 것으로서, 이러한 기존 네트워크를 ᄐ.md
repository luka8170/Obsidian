## Key Offshore Assets  
해양 오프쇼어는 5대 주요 자산군을 중심으로 구성됨

### 1. Drilling Units  
- 시추선, 드릴 파이프, 드릴 비트 등  
- Azimuth thruster 포함

### 2. Floating Units  
- 반잠수식(Semi-Sub), SPAR, FPSO, TLP 등  
- 생산 설비(Top sides) 장착됨

### 3. Subsea Assets  
- 리서(Risers), 플로우라인, 엄빌리컬(Umbilicals)  
- 해저 생산 시스템

### 4. Fixed Platforms  
- 상부 생산설비가 있는 고정식 플랫폼

### 5. Windmills  
- 해상 풍력 터빈 설비  

> **참고:** BV Marine Division은 Offshore 사업 통합을 위해 BV Marine & Offshore Division으로 명칭 변경됨.

## Offshore References Highlights

### Major Historic Projects

- First classed FPSO: Castellon Delta (Shell & SBM, 1979)  
- LPG-FGSO: NKS2 (N’KOSSA), First purpose-built FSO (1996)  
- Others: IFRIKA II (Tunisia, 1979), NAN HAI XI WANG (1986), Petrobras XIX (1997)  
- Renowned FPSOs: Plutonio, Girassol, Akpo, Dalia, Alima, Unity, Usan  

### Selected New Building FPSO and Production Units

- TOTAL FPSO Clov, Shell Prelude FLNG  
- Petrobras P55, Total FPSO Egina  
- Others: MOHO BILONDO FPU, PAZFLOR FPSO, CENDOR FSO  

### FPSO/FPU Class & Technical Assistance

- BV classes or studies >130 FPSOs/FSOs, representing 50% of fleet  
- BV leader in fatigue analysis, hydrodynamics, mooring (Statoil projects)  
- IVB services for ENI Goliat FPSO (Norway)  

### Independent Verification & Certification Examples

- FPU P-53 (Brazil): 25 years operation, 1080m water depth, 180,000 bbls/day oil production  
- FPSO-BR P-57: double side design, 180,000 bopd, 1,850,000 barrels storage, certification work over 5 months  

### TLWP / SPAR Platforms

- Design checks and verification for Campos Basin dry completion floating units  
- Tension Leg Wellhead Platform and Spar-buoy type platforms
