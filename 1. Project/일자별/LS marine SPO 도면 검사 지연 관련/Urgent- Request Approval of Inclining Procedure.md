We don't agree with you that the free-flow of water obstructed by the longi. Girder is minor impact. In general, inclining test by use of ballast water is not recommended as there are many factors that will affect the accuracy of the test results, therefore we have to pay more attention to the test procedure. The inclining moment is created by the shifting of the water, it is achieved by calculating the TCG of water inside the tanks after each water transfer and ship inclined, and the TCG of water will be changed as the ship heeled at each step. Therefore any obstruction to the water free-flow will result in the final KG under estimated.
