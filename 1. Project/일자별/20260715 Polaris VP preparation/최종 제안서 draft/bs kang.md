We have had a meeting with Polaris Busan office (technical) and met with Executive Director Jung Yong-gi, Team Leader Min Seong-il during which we discussed the 210K Ore Carriers for Vale.

It was confirmed that Polaris is in discussion with Qingdao Beihai. The contract is not final yet, but Polaris is planning 4 vessels. Mentioning our interest to be the Class and explaining our intention to consider this project as Premium, Mr. Jung asked BV to prepare a formal offer which must include how to handle any future class-related issues, how the site office will be organized, how the site inspections will be done, and FYF terms after delivery. It was mentioned that Polaris may consider either KR or BV as single class or another option could be dual class KR with ABS or LR or DNV.

Another topic addressed by Mr. Jung was the loss of their vessel Stellar Daisy in 2017, noting some concerns about how it was handled at the time by KR. On this, Polaris has requested BV to explore the possibility of further enhancing the rule requirements applicable to VLOCs. We are in contact with HO for this, and are waiting data’s from Polaris side.

Besides Vale project, Mr. Jung informed that Polaris is under active discussion with Hengli SY to acquire 2 Newcastlemax BC’s contracted by Hengli affiliated entity. Drawing approval has already been done with ABS. However, he informed that the construction is not yet started and these vessels could still go to BV if Hengli accepts. [@Bill CHEN](mailto:bill.chen@bureauveritas.com), could you investigate and confirm that MOU can apply. We will do same on our side for NS. [@Mustafa BILGIN](mailto:mustafa.bilgin@bureauveritas.com), could you revert with an organization site, survey involvement, and consider this prospect as Premium.

Other considerations:

- HMM:

HMM being a state-owned company controlled by Korea Development Bank (KDB) and being subject to annual parliamentary audit, only considers KR. Choosing a foreign class can have serious consequences for HMM management if this is not thoroughly justified. Previous CEO has been replaced for such choice.

- Wooyang Shipping (mentioned by Bill in another email):

Established in 1982, they have traditionally been a dedicated carrier for POSCO's steel products (utilizing Handymax/Supramax General Cargo vessels & Handysize bulkers, around 10k–55k DWT). In April 2025, they officially entered the raw ore transportation market by acquiring two 325k DWT VLOCs (Wooyang Moria and Wooyang Erebor) from SK Shipping, along with their existing long-term Vale CVC (Consecutive Voyage Charter) contracts.

Their primary charterer, POSCO, is a quasi-governmental entity in South Korea. Like HMM, POSCO strongly discourages the use of foreign classification societies to ship operators such as Wooyang.

In the past, approached them to explore potential cooperation, but they firmly maintained a 100% KR-only. Anyway, we have approached them again. They confirmed that the class for this Vale project, should they order vessels, has already been decided, and there is no room for change. They did not specify which class, but given their entire fleet is currently KR, it seems almost certain that KR is the one. We will keep monitoring for any updates.
