1. Purposes OF THE AGREEMENT

The purpose of this Agreement is to set up a framework for collaboration between the Parties to:

- Define the conditions under which BVS and its Affiliates undertakes to perform the Services listed in Appendix 1 on behalf of SK Shipping, and in particular the nature, prices, and deadlines for carrying out these Services.
- Simplify the contractual and financial terms between the Parties as part of the performance of the Services.
- Guarantee the confidentiality of the data exchanged between the Parties in the context of the performance of the Services, and the discussions preceding it.

1. Duration

This Agreement shall become effective upon its date of signature and remain in force until ‘discounted amount’ is spent out (hereinafter: the “**Term**”) thereafter, whereupon it shall expire unless terminated earlier or extended by mutual agreement of the Parties.

The Parties shall meet within two (2) months preceding the expiration of the Agreement to discuss the principles and conditions of possible renewal. Any tacit renewal is expressly excluded.

1. general provisions

The Parties undertake to cooperate fully for the proper performance of the Agreement and will consult each other regularly in order to assess the quality of the Services and to take corrective measures if necessary.

It is up to the Parties to take all necessary measures with regard to their respective personnel, to facilitate the performance of the Services throughout the duration of the Agreement, in particular:

► Ensure the availability, cooperation, and competence of its staff;

► Consult each other without delay to deal with any unforeseen situations which would require arbitration;

► Provide the other Party, in a timely manner, with all the elements necessary for it to be able to perform the Services within the deadlines;

► Ensure the proper functioning of the follow-up meetings, maintaining at all times a representative for each of the Parties, as defined in Article 14.

As from the submission of the final Deliverable, SK Shipping has a period of ten (10) working days to examine the Deliverable submitted. At the end of this period, SK Shipping formally validates receipt of the Deliverable or sends its consolidated comments to BVS. Upon receipt of these comments, BVS makes the necessary corrections and/or clarifications to submit the updated Deliverable to SK Shipping within five (5) working days.

SK Shipping has an additional period of five (5) working days from the date of new delivery of the Deliverable to accept the Deliverable. Past this period of five (5) days, SK Shipping is deemed to have accepted the Deliverable, therefore SK Shipping shall not be able to make any further comments or demands regarding the Deliverable.
