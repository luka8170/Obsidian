## Executive Summary

Bureau Veritas has been a pioneer in LNG carrier classification since 1965, offering world-class competence in membrane containment systems and cargo handling systems. This annex demonstrates BV's comprehensive expertise, proven track record with GTT membrane systems, and technical capabilities essential for the successful delivery and operation of the Hyundai Glovis 174K LNGC project.

## 1. BV Track Record in LNG Carrier Classification

### 1.1 Historical Leadership

BV is a leading class society, founding member of the International Association of Classification Societies and is deeply involved in the LNG distribution chain including the classification of LNG carriers since 1965 with the 25,000 m³ **JULES VERNE**.

### 1.2 Current Fleet Statistics

**BV has a proven record and long experience in the classification of LNG carriers. We believe that LNG has a role to play as a transition fuel on the route to zero carbon.**

To-date, **246** LNG carriers have been classed by BV out of which **128** are units currently in service (including 8 LNG bunkering ships built in The Republic of Korea, China and Europe), **3** are under conversion and **69** are on order (including 4 LNG bunkering ships at several yards in the Republic of Korea, Singapore, China and Spain). Our reference list of LNG carriers also includes **16** ships classed after construction with BV (transfer of class from another class society), and a few other ships transferred to other class societies or recycled.

## 3. Propulsion System Integration

### 3.1 Gas-Fueled Engine Experience

BV has considerable experience with gas fuelled engines and more specifically 4 stroke (DFDE) and 2 stroke (X-DF) propulsion, being the pioneers with:

- **DFDE:** 75,000 m³ **GLOBAL ENERGY** delivered in 2006
- **X-DF:** 180,000 m³ **SK AUDACE** delivered in 2017

### 3.2 DFDE Propulsion Leadership

Since 2006, **55** LNG carriers with DFDE propulsion have been built with BV class. BV has type approved the dual fuel engines proposed by the major manufacturers in particular MAN, WÄRTSILÄ and HiMSEN usually installed on these types of ship.

### 3.3 X-DF Technology Leadership

In particular, BV is a leading classification society in the segment of X-DF LNG carriers with around **53** units built or under construction equipped with this type of twin screw propulsion system. 

More recently our classification society has been the first to work on the certification of the brand-new **MAN B&W 6G70ME-C10.5-GA** for part of the Dynagas orders of 200,000 m³ capacity LNG carriers at HHI.

**SK AUDACE** - First ever Mark III Flex 180,000 m³ with 4 tanks and X-DF in the world (2017)

## 4. Reliquefaction and BOG Management Systems

### 4.1 Modern Reliquefaction Technology

BV is also involved in the classification of LNG carriers equipped with brand new reliquefaction or sub-cooling systems:

**Babcock ecoSMRT® Technology:**
The first two LNG carriers with Babcock ecoSMRT® technology are classed by BV. The ships have been recently delivered by HHI and HSHI respectively in 2020 and more are on order.

**HANWHA OCEAN Technology:**
Other full or partial reliquefaction technologies developed by HANWHA OCEAN (formerly DSME) have been approved by BV and are presently installed on LNG carriers with BV class.

The modern ALAT (Air Liquide Advanced Technologies) sub-cooling Turbo Brayton system has been approved by BV and is presently installed on LNG carriers with BV class.

A total of around **90** LNG carriers under BV class are fitted with or will be fitted with any of such modern technologies.

## 9. Technical Analysis Capabilities
