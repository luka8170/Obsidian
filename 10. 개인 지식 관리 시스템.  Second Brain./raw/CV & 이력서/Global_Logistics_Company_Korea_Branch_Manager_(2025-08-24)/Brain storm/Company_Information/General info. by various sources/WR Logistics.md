## Industries we serve  

Specialized expertise across industrial and commercial sectors:  

- **Petrochemicals** – Safe, compliant logistics for complex chemical cargo and specialized project movements.  
- **Oil & gas** – Transport solutions for upstream, midstream, and downstream operations in challenging environments.  
- **Energy** – Heavy-lift logistics and equipment transport for power generation and infrastructure projects.  
- **Mining & metals** – Transport of heavy machinery and materials for extraction and processing operations.  
- **Pulp & paper** – Efficient movement of bulk materials, machinery, and production components.  
- **Life sciences** – Controlled logistics for temperature-sensitive and regulated shipments.  
- **Automotive** – Logistics for industrial-scale vehicle production and supply chain operations.  
- **Retail** – Execution of complex, time-sensitive commercial logistics projects.  
- **Events** – Time-critical logistics for global exhibitions, launches, and event infrastructure delivery.
