| **Meeting minutes**                                                                                                                                                | Attendees <br><br>- YOCEAN / Ventus Marine Korea:  BH Park(CEO), MS Park(Director)<br>- BV: CE, SJ Han<br><br>1. Company & Business Overview<br><br>- Ventus Marine Korea is the third joint venture of PSA Marine, following the UK and Taiwan.<br><br>- YOCEAN is operating its first CTV and plans to deliver the second CTV by July 14, 2025.<br><br>2. CTV Market Outlook & Strategy<br><br>- The Korean government is promoting CTV localization projects targeting 2028.<br><br>- Multiple consortiums are preparing for government-supported tenders.<br><br>- Several Korean shipyards are converting facilities to build aluminum CTVs.<br><br>3. BV’s Role & Collaboration<br><br>- YeoSu Ocean emphasized that the current CTV drawing approval process being handled by BV Singapore is causing delays and inconvenience for Korean shipbuilders, making the overall process less efficient.<br><br>- They pointed out that the lack of delegated engineers in BV Korea with the necessary authorization is a key obstacle to streamlining domestic CTV development.<br><br>- They acknowledged BV’s explanation that delegation rights require a certain number of completed projects and specialized experience, which is currently concentrated in Singapore.<br><br>- Nonetheless, YeoSu Ocean urged BV to accelerate the training and delegation of Korean engineers in anticipation of growing local demand, so that drawing approvals can be handled within Korea in the near future.<br><br>- YeoSu Ocean welcomed BV’s stated commitment to supporting Korean shipbuilders and owners through enhanced technical cooperation and guidance.<br><br>4. BV Korea Office Operations<br><br>- Head office in Busan, with satellite offices in Seoul, Incheon, Mokpo, and Ulsan.<br><br>- Actively participating in Korean government marine projects.<br><br>- BV Solutions provides advisory services, risk assessments, and technical support.<br><br>5. Safety & Labor Discussions<br><br>- Concerns raised regarding increased safety incidents in major Korean shipyards.<br><br>- YOCEAN emphasized its strong safety culture, with regular training and strict procedures.<br><br>6. Future Collaboration Suggestions<br><br>- Request for BV to streamline the CTV approval process for Korean shipbuilders.<br><br>- Proposal to organize a CTV owners’ workshop or seminar.<br><br>- BV confirmed willingness to support shipyards through technical guidance and engineering cooperation. |                                                             |                         |     |     |      |
