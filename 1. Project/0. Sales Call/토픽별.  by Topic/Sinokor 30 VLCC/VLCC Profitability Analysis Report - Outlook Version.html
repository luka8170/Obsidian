Key Insights

1. Serious Profitability Issues in FYF Contracts

  • All VLCCs under FYF contracts are rated "Loss-making"
  • PV Total ranges from 0.03 to 0.11 - extremely low performance
    • This means only 3-11% of target achievement
  • FYF contracts are severely underperforming financial expectations

2. Strong Performance in ISM/MLC Contracts

  • Vessels 40669H and 40670J show "High" profitability in ISM/MLC contracts
  • PV Total above 2.0 - exceeding 200% of target
  • ISM/MLC contracts effectively offset some FYF losses

3. Fleet Age Profile

  • Fleet age: 3.67 to 6.58 years - relatively young vessels
  • Despite being modern tonnage, FYF contracts still show poor profitability
  • Age is not the issue - pricing structure is the problem

Conclusions and Recommendations

Current Situation

✅ ISM/MLC contracts with dual coverage perform well (40669H, 40670J)
❌ FYF-only vessels show severe losses (34861U, 34862V, 41425E, 42044C)
⚠️ Overall VLCC portfolio is unprofitable with discount rate of 112%

Strategic Implications for 30 VLCC Opportunity

Given the current profitability challenges:

  1. Contract Structure: Any new VLCC contracts should consider lessons learned from existing portfolio
  2. Pricing Strategy: Current FYF pricing model clearly needs adjustment
  3. Multi-Service Approach: ISM/MLC additions improve overall profitability
  4. Risk Assessment: Securing 30 VLCCs at similar terms could significantly increase losses

Recommendation

A comprehensive pricing review is essential before committing to the 30 VLCC opportunity. While winning this business is strategically important, the current contract terms demonstrate that volume alone does not guarantee profitability.
