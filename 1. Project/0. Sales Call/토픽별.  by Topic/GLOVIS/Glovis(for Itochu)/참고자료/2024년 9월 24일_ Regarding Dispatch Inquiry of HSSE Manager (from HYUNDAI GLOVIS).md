We sincerely appreciate your consideration of BV for your classification needs.

BV fully recognizes the importance of HSE measures for the successful execution of the QE project. BV is diligently implementing these HSE activities in the ongoing QE 1st batch as well.

Please be informed that, in accordance with EMSA regulations and recently adopted BV M&O internal processes, BVS (BV Solutions), the technical support entity of BV Marine &Offshore, is strictly prohibited from engaging in PMC activities for BV classed vessels. This EU regulation is uniformly applicable for all EU RO’s. Therefore, the HSE Manager from BVS will not be able to participate in requested PMC activities.

Given these regulations, BV is committed to providing you with the utmost benefit through the following proposal:

**BV’s Commitment to Supporting HSE Activities During Construction  
**BV well understands the importance of HSE in this project.  Our site Office Surveyors including BV HSE coordinators and HSE Manager are fully prepared to collaborate and support HSE activities during the construction period.

**Proposed HSE Support and Compliance Plan without any extra cost**

Under the situation that we have no detailed information of your HSE activities and our survey fee can’t cover the cost of international qualified HSE surveyor, we would like to propose that our HSSE coordinators and HSSE Manager in our site team assist your HSE manager in your site team to comply with the requirement from the charterer and they will proceed this HSE collaboration and support in accordance with BV’s utmost HSE standard without any extra cost.

**QHSE & Operation Director's Regular Site Visits and HSE Training/Inspections without any extra cost**

In addition to above, our QHSE & Operation Director covering North Asia Zone will visit the site regularly and offer HSE trainings and inspections as required by the owner without any extra cost.

We would like to take this opportunity to emphasize that BV, with its strengths in LNGC shipbuilding and operations is an indispensable business partner for your company.

Thank you for considering our proposal. We look forward to the opportunity to work closely with you.
