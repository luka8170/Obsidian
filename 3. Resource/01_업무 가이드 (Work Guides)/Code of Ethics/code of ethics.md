**Facilitation payments**[close](https://bureauveritas.plateau.com/icontent_e/CUSTOM_rot/bureauveritas/self-managed/PROD/IDBureauVeritas_default_3/en/index1.htm#)

These are payments made to officials with the intention of expediting or ensuring the smooth performance of an administrative process to which the payer is legally entitled. In most countries, facilitation payments are prohibited by law and are considered as bribes.

**Influence peddling**[close](https://bureauveritas.plateau.com/icontent_e/CUSTOM_rot/bureauveritas/self-managed/PROD/IDBureauVeritas_default_3/en/index1.htm#)

This is a type of corruption that involves providing a benefit to a third party in exchange for exerting influence over a government official.

**Political contributions**[close](https://bureauveritas.plateau.com/icontent_e/CUSTOM_rot/bureauveritas/self-managed/PROD/IDBureauVeritas_default_3/en/index1.htm#)

This is any contribution, whether in cash or in kind, that supports a political cause, party, candidate or official.


GIFTS, HOSPITALITY AND ENTERTAINMENT GIVEN AND RECEIVED

No gift, hospitality or entertainment should be given or received if it could potentially influence a business decision.

Hospitality and entertainment, including participation in trade fairs and similar professional events which are sponsored by suppliers, are acceptable but they are restricted and subject to reporting and clearance obligations.

**We must**

- Request approval from our manager before **accepting** any gift above a certain value per gift (or on a cumulative basis per person per year). Consult the gifts guidelines or your Compliance officer.

- Seek the Group Compliance Officer's approval before **offering** any gift to, or paying travel and accommodation for any client or business partner, which exceeds €150 (or in excess of €300 on a cumulative basis per person in one calendar year).

- Book accurately the payment of any gift hospitality and entertainment given or received.

**We must not**

- Accept or offer gifts or entertainment to get someone to do or refrain from doing something (or to give the impression of having done so).

- Accept or offer payments of cash, tips, loans or cash equivalent gifts from or to suppliers or clients.

- Accept gifts from suppliers when we are involved in procurement negotiations or the selection of suppliers.
