### What if I haven’t received my booking confirmation?

Most bookings are confirmed straight away, so please check your spam folder if there’s nothing in your inbox.

You can always check the status of your booking online:

**If you’re using the app**, tap ‘Bookings’ at the bottom of the screen.

**If you’re using the website**, make sure you’re signed in and on the ‘Car rentals’ page - then hit ‘Manage booking’ at the top.

**What’s the status?**

If you find that your booking isn’t confirmed yet, we’re working on it - and we’ll email you as soon as it is. In the unlikely event that we run into any problems, we’ll let you know as soon as we can.

### Will I have to pay for anything when I pick up my car?

The main driver will need to present a credit card in their own name to cover the security deposit. Very few rental companies accept debit cards for the deposit - please check the car’s rental terms.

They’ll also charge you for:

1. any extras (GPS, child seats, etc.)

2. any fees (young driver fee, one-way fee, etc.)

3. any additional cover that you buy from them.

### What if I need to cancel my booking?

If you cancel **more than** 48 hours before your rental is due to start, you’ll receive a full refund*.

If you cancel **less than** 48 hours before, or while you’re at the rental counter at pick-up time, we’ll refund* what you paid _minus_ the cost of 3 days of your rental - so there won’t be any refund if your car was booked for 3 days or less.

If you cancel **after** your rental is due to start (or you just don’t turn up) you’ll receive no refund.

_* Unless you’ve booked a car that’s labelled ‘non-refundable’._

### What if I want to take my rental car to another country?

First of all, there may be a fee for this.

**Picking your car up in one country and dropping it off in another?**

1. If you’re booking online, just enter your pick-up location **and** drop-off location on our homepage.

2. If you’re booking on the phone, just tell us.

**Just visiting another country - then coming back to the pick-up location?**

1. If you’re booking online, open the car’s rental terms, go to the ‘Extra Services (Payable at Counter)’ section, and check out ‘Cross-Border’ and ‘Boundary rules’.

2. If you’re booking on the phone, just tell us.

3. Either way, please check with the counter staff at pick-up as well.

### What is the Price Match Guarantee and how does it work?

If you’ve booked a car with us, then found the same deal for less somewhere else, we want to know!

To benefit from our Price Match Guarantee, the main driver just needs to give us a call using the phone number on our Contact us page and tell us the details, including:

1. the booking number

2. the name of the website with the cheaper deal

3. the price for the cheaper deal.

We’ll check the details, and if it meets our Terms and Conditions, we’ll refund the difference to the same payment card that was used to make the original booking.

{start_bold}Terms and Conditions{end_bold}

Our Price Match Guarantee applies to like-for-like prepaid rental deals offered by other independent car rental operators.  

It is available only after booking with us and before the rental’s start date.   

It does not apply to deals that are part of a promotional offer with the other operator, or to insurance products.

Please note that ‘like-for-like’ means comparable in terms of car type, pick-up and drop-off dates, location(s), terms and conditions, and any relevant fees (including additional driver fees) or extra equipment. For example, if the other operator’s price is for a rental with the same pick-up and drop-off locations, but our price includes a one-way fee, this will not be considered like-for-like.
