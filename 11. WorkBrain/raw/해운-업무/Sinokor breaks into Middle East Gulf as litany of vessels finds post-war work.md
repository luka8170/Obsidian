Sinokor has nearly two dozen VLCCs in the Middle East Gulf, many of them apparently part of the new Strait of Hormuz shuttle service.

MB Shipbrokers said AIS shows that the giant South Korean shipowner has at least 22 VLCCs in the Gulf, or 25% of available tonnage in the region.

The Danish broking house said Sinokor has been “highly active” in the shuttle service, in which more daring owners send tankers to lift crude in the Gulf and exit through the Strait of Hormuz to offload to ships controlled by more risk-averse companies.

“This suggests willingness to transit is still concentrated among a few risk-tolerant owners rather than broadly shared across the market,” MB Shipbrokers said.

A few ships appear to be making most of the shuttle transits, a practice that began before the 18 June reopening of the strait.

Sinokor’s 317,400-dwt Cameroon Prosperity (built 2007) has made three voyages in the past month, loading twice in the United Arab Emirates before passing the cargoes on to ships linked to Frontline and DHT Holdings outside the Gulf.

Other Sinokor vessels, which do not seem to be used in the shuttle trades, look to be headed to the Iraqi port of Basrah.

Iraq is reportedly seeking higher production quotas and TotalEnergies is said to be marketing its oil at discount prices in Asia.

The 300,000-dwt South Africa Prosperity (built 2011) is broadcasting that it is sailing from Dubai to Basrah.

Its last port call appears to be Sikka in north-west India after loading at Fujairah.

On Friday, Clarksons’ assessment for VLCCs sailing from the Middle East Gulf to China was $285,800 per day, down 3.6% from last week and nearly 30% from last month.

During the week, Fearnleys said owners had done well to keep rates elevated, but said their resolve “is [starting to show cracks](https://www.tradewindsnews.com/tankers/vlcc-rates-hit-as-cracks-emerge-in-owners-resolve-brokers-say/2-1-2012369) ” as volumes have subsided.

“\[Volumes\] are unlikely to see much improvement for the remainder of the 60-day memorandum of understanding between the US and Iran,” the Norwegian broker said.

MB Shipbrokers forecast rising competition in the region, as VLCCs appear to be ballasting towards the Middle East and no longer to the Atlantic basin.

[Sinokor was one of the more aggressive owners](https://www.tradewindsnews.com/tankers/as-tankers-trickle-out-of-middle-east-gulf-sinokor-vlccs-start-pushing-in/2-1-2008325) in sending VLCCs into the Gulf of Oman in hopes of being among the first to lift cargoes from exporters inside the Middle East Gulf once the Strait of Hormuz reopened.

In early June, as the US-Iran war dragged on, it had at least 14 vessels in the region, easily outpacing competitors [who had three or fewer](https://www.tradewindsnews.com/tankers/sinokor-poised-to-cash-in-from-strait-of-hormuz-reopening-as-tankers-flock-to-region/2-1-2001053).

### 상황 해석

**호르무즈 셔틀 서비스**
미-이란 전쟁 종전 후 호르무즈가 재개방되면서 새로운 2단계 운항 패턴 등장. 시노코르 등 리스크 허용 선주가 걸프 내에서 원유 선적 → 해협 밖에서 Frontline·DHT 연계 선박으로 화물 이송. MB Shipbrokers: *"셔틀 의지가 소수 선주에 집중, 시장 전반에 확산되지 않은 상태"*

**이라크 바스라 루트**
이라크 증산 쿼터 추구 + TotalEnergies 아시아 할인 판매 → 시노코르 일부 선박 바스라 직항.

**운임 하락 압력**
- 미-이란 MOU(60일) 기간 물동량 회복 제한적
- 대서양 향하던 VLCC들이 중동으로 복귀 → 공급 경쟁 심화
- Fearnleys: *"선주들의 운임 방어 의지에 균열 시작"*

### BV Korea 영업 활용 포인트

1. **시노코르 VLCC 집중 운용**: 걸프 22척 중 셔틀 반복 운항 선박(Cameroon Prosperity 등)은 단기간 고강도 운항 → **ISM/ISPS 컴플라이언스 점검, 임시 검사 수요** 가능성
2. **리스크 구역 운항 이력**: 호르무즈 셔틀 운항 선박은 전쟁 중 위험 구역 진입 이력 → 보험사 요구 추가 검사(Condition Survey 등) 발생 여지
3. **이라크 바스라 루트**: 시노코르 선박 바스라 기항 → BV 이라크 거점과 연계 가능한 선박 입항 검사 기회
4. **운임 하락기 대응**: 선주들이 비용 절감 압력받는 시기 → BV 통합계약(CAC) 패키지 제안 적기
