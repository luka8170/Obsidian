1. Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive owner marketing and sales strategy for BV Korea to achieve its 2024 goals. It
outlines specitic strategies for customer retention and acquisition to address major trends such as IMO regulatory compliance, the expansion of ESG management, and accelerated digital transformation. The objective is to achieve an annual 5% growth in Ns and Nc feet GT and ensure sustainable management through the acquisition of new
clients.
