> 3.       The following points hindered our work :
> 
> 1.       Initial submitted design has been subject to numerous comments. We would like to point out that on the 227 drawings currently reviewed, 1930 comments were issued, i.e. an average of comments per drawings exceeding 8 which is unusual. Huge effort has been  dedicated to the project to clarify and explain the requirements to the Yard so that eventually the Vessel is compliant with applicable rules and reaches from that perspective a satisfactory level of safety and integrity.
> 
> 2.       Despite efforts on both sides, comments have been proven difficult to resolve as yard has often proposed solutions not evidently compliant with the rules, generating further discussions.
> 
> 3.       Updates of the drawings generally corresponded to design development not necessarily due to our comments.
> 
> 4.       The SRTP principles delivered during the training at early stage of the training were not followed by the Yard, opening the way to extensive discussions on the matter. The SRtP concept was submitted four times (last revision early this month).
> 
> 4.       Be aware that SRtP analysis shall be supported by the Vessel drawings to have a comprehensive review. The SRtP analysis is to be consistent with the available drawings. This took a very long time to reach this status. For instance, the active fire protection drawings were received for the first time at the end of last month, i.e. very late in the process. Until we have the complete package, the review cannot be finalized, and therefore we are in a position to only issue preliminary comments that are likely to be revisited because of concurrent design development.
> 
> 5.       3 366 manhours have been spent for hull structure compared to a budget of 1 399 manhours, hence an overrun of 240% for a project that is not completed, which again shows our dedication.
