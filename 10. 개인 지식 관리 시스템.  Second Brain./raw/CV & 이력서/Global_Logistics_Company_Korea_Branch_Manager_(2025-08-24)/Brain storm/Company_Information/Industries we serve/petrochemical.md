
# Logistics for petrochemical projects and operations

WR Logistics provides logistics services for petrochemical projects, including construction, ongoing operations, and the handling of sensitive equipment and materials.

## Logistics support for critical petrochemical components

WR Logistics manages the safe transport, storage, and delivery of petrochemical equipment and materials for construction, maintenance, and operations, with support tailored to industry needs.

Key Features:

- Transport of oversized refinery and processing equipment
- QHSES-compliant logistics for petrochemical components
- Coordinated logistics for time-critical plant turnarounds and shutdowns
- Customs support for complex petrochemical imports
- Project management for plant construction and expansion
