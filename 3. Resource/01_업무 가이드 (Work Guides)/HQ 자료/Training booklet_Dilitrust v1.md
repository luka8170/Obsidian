## Why Dilitrust

A contract management solution that facilitates the generation, drafting, negotiation, validation, and signature of contracts.
This tool allows centralized and secure storage of contracts with search and reporting capabilities.

### Contract Process Flow:

**Standard Contract template**
- We select the library's verified template.

**First draft of the contract**
- Just a few details must be submitted to create the contract's initial version.

**Negotiation and Renegotiation**
- The tool's timetable management feature will make these procedures precise and efficient.

**Signature**
- Thanks to the workflow and electronic signature function, contracts are finalised rapidly.

## How to create a contract

1. Click on 'draft from template' to start a new contract
2. Click on 'single document' to initiate the process

### Contract Creation Steps:

**Client Name Selection**
- The 'FYF document' must be selected in the document type.

**CD Information & Client Information**
- Fill in required client details and CD information

**Document Editing**
1. Click on 'Word icon' (edit document) to add additional information on surveys, ships, amount etc.
2. Based on preference, choose between working on a desktop or online.

**Document Completion**
1. The customer and BV office information is prefilled on the template
2. To make it easy to review additions or deletions from the Standard contract, please start the track changes
3. Once we have completed the other fields in the contract, save and close the word document
4. Return to the Dilitrust tool and refresh the page to observe the modifications.

## How to send a document for Internal and External review

1. Internal review must be completed before sending the document for external review
2. You can add more than one reviewer
3. An email notification is sent to the reviewer as soon as the contract is sent for review
4. Reviewer has the option to approve or reject the request and provide suggestions for revisions
5. This procedure can be repeated more than once
6. A similar process has to be employed for external reviews.

## How to complete the summary sheet

To complete the summary sheet, first click the validate/sign button.

Review information in the right window to complete the summary.

### Summary Sheet Fields:

**Basic Contract Information:**
- **Effective date** - Start date of the contract or term
- **Expiration date** - Date of contract end
- **Date of signature** – Contract Signature date
- **Contract duration date** – Write the contract duration
- **Duration condition** - How does the contract have to be renewed
- **Termination Clause** – Select the termination clauses in the contract
- **Contract ended** – Contract status, For active contract no, for closed yes

**FYF Specific Information:**
- **FYF scope** – Select the correct scope (NS,ISM,ISPS..)
- **FYF Country** – CD country
- **Amount** – Total contract amount and currency
- **Occasional survey** – Excluded from standard fees (Y/N)
- **Overtime clause** – excluded from standard fees (Y/N)
- **Price revision mechanism** – standard or other

**Additional Contract Details:**
- **Inflation index** – Select detail about the inflation index
- **Annex** - Select the appropriate information from the contract
- **Installment amount** – Installment amount and duration
- **General Annex** – IF general condition annex added select 'Yes'
- **General condition** – If general condition are modified select modified if not then standard

**If General conditions are not standard the following elements will appear:**
- **Term of payment** – Frequency of the payment
- **Limitation of liability** – Liability or contractual provision included in the contract
- **Force Majeure** – Force majeure event provision to be selected
- **Governing law** – Law provision that determines which law shall apply in the event of a dispute
- **Dispute resolution** – Information about the dispute resolution
- **Professional ethics** – Details about ethics

### Important Notes:
- If a certain information is not included in the contract, choose "Information is missing"
- To complete the summary for an attribute that is missing, you have two options: either use the search icon to get the relevant information or choose the appropriate phrase or the amount from the contract directly, then input the attribute's name, and tool will suggest correct attribute which can be selected.
- Completing the summary is crucial. It is possible to modify a summary.

## How to send a document for signature

Additional signatory can be added by clicking on the '+' sign below.

### Important Signature Notes:

1. The client contact person is able to be added as a signatory. The signatory will only receive an email upon the completion of validation, and the client will only receive an email seeking signing in the validated and signed internal contract once the document has been signed by the BV signatory.

2. Additionally, while sending the request, it's a good idea to select the appropriate signature area.

3. You can select the appropriate signature location and person by using the drop-down menu. then you may designate a location for other details like the date and email address.

© Copyright Bureau Veritas

## How the reviewer will review the contract

Once the document is submitted for review, reviewers receive an email with a link to open the document and can:
- Accept the contract
- Edit the document or comment for changes
- Delete the workflow if the person is not responsible to approve the contract

© Copyright Bureau Veritas

## How the validator will approve the contract

Validator will receive an email from the contract creator when they submit a contract for validation, and we can click "View Document" to review the relevant contract.

Validator can validate or refuse the contract.

© Copyright Bureau Veritas

## How signatory will sign the contract

### Signing Process:

1. Signatory will receive an email from the contract creator when they submit a contract for signature, and we can click "review document" to review and sign the relevant contract.

2. Click Continue to begin the review.

3. There are several suggestions for adding a signature, or it may be uploaded or drawn.

4. Click "Finish" once the contract has been signed and further information has been provided, such as a date and email. then a notification regarding the modified status will be sent to the creator.

5. Even the client must sign the document in this manner.

## Additional functionality
