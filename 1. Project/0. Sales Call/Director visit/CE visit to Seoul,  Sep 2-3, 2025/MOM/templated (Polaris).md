1. Business Background & Fleet Status
   - Polaris Group operates 39 vessels (mainly bulk carriers: 22 ore carriers, 16 bulk carriers)
   - Current BV classification: 1 vessel only (Solar Majesty, 2020, 180,516 DWT)
   - BV market share: ~2.5% of Polaris fleet
   - Active orderbook: 16 VLOCs at Hyundai Heavy Industries (325,000 DWT, delivery 2025-2027)

2. Container Ship Projects - SUSPENDED
   - Original plans: 2,700 TEU feeders (Huanghai Shipyard) + 1,900 TEU feeders (Huangpu-Wenchong)
   - Suspension reasons (Chairman Han's explanation):
     • Geopolitical uncertainty affecting investment confidence
     • Market oversupply: excessive newbuilding orders in past 3-4 months
     • Large capital requirements during uncertain times
     • Strategic "wait and see" approach until market stabilizes

3. Strategic Focus: Wind Propulsion Technology
   - Chairman Han expressed significant interest in wind-assisted propulsion as strategic direction
   - Post-meeting email from GIGA Director Park specified requirements:
     • Container carriers (LOLO): 300-1,000 TEU
     • RoRo/Ropax: LOA 150-250m, Beam 25-35m
     • Target: 40-90% wind power contribution under predetermined conditions

4. Chairman's Research Interest & Reference Materials
   - Discussed global wind propulsion examples:
     • Pyxis Ocean: bulk carrier achieving 30% wind propulsion with WindWings
     • French projects: Canopée (Ariane 6 transport), Neoliner Origin (commercial RoRo)
     • Container applications: Windcoop (210 TEU), CWS Airfin350 technology
   - Performance benchmarks: 20-90% fuel savings, 60-90% CO₂ reduction
