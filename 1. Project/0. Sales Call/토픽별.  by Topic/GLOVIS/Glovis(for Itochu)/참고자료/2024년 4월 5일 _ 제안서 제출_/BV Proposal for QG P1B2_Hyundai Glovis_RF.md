About Bureau Veritas Marine & Offshore

Founded in 1828, today Bureau Veritas Group (BV), Paris headquartered, has more than 82,000

employees in nearly 1,600 offices and laboratories world-wide. Bv is a world leader in laboratory testing,

inspection and certification. Pioneering the safety and performance of new technologies, BV is also one

of the world’s leading ship & offshore classitication and verification societies, with a global network to

support clients worldwide.

With a fleet of more than 11,500 ships and offshore units classed, BV is the largest classification society

by number of assets. The diverse fleet covers all major merchant shipping segments - bulkers, tankers,

container ships, passenger ships, offshore service vessels, tugs, dredgers, naval ships and other specialized

and sophisticated ship types as well, of course, as being a leader in gas carriers and LNG activities across

the marine supply chain.

In addition to core classification services BV also offers services beyond class, including technical advisory.

asset management and assurance solutions, as well as marine fuel and lubricating oil testing.

with 2,800 people in the Marine & Offshore division, Bureau Veritas engineers and technical experts have

the world-leading expertise and hands on industry experience to support the safety and performance of

our clients’ ships and offshore assets. The global Bv Marine and Offshore network comprises 180 survey

stations, 6 marine operations centers and 16 plan approval offices.

Leaders in LNG carrier classification

operations throughout the development of the carriage of LNG by sea. Today BV has substantial and a

leading market shares across the marine LNG supply chain including the most modern LNG carriers, new

FSRUs / FSUs and the growing market for LNG bunker vessels- and more.

As pioneers in LNG classification, BV has the knowledge to class all types of liquefied natural gas carriers.

BY experts with bespoke software tools are fully prepared to address all challenges and support industry

2. BV Track records of LNG carriers

BV is a leading class society, founding member of the International Association of Classification Societies

and is deeply involved in the LNG distribution chain including the classification of LNG carriers since 1965

with the 25,000 m JULES VERNE.

BV has a proven record and long experience in the classification of LNG carriers. We believe that

LNG has a role to play as a transition fuel on the route to zero carbon.

To-date, 246 LNG carriers have been classed by BV out of which 128 are units currently in service (including

8 LNG bunkering ships built in The Republic of Korea, China and Europe), 3 are under conversion and 69 are on order (including 4 LNG bunkering ships at several vards in the Republic of Korea, Singapore, China and Spain). Our reference list of LNG carriers also includes 16 ships classed after construction with Bv

(transfer of class from another class society), and a few other ships transferred to other class societies or

recycled.

With reference to LNG containment systems BV has been engaged in the examination of multiple systems

and has given its approval to the major ones including the GTT membrane systems (mainly NO and Mark)

and the independent type B systems such as Moss and IHI SPB. In particular the latest technology NO96

Super+ has been assessed and is to be installed for the first time ever in 4 LNG carriers to be built in

HANWHA OCEAN. We are involved in the classification of the largest LNG carriers with both GTT and Moss

type containment systems with 4 cargo tanks, respectively SK AUDACE (180,000 m3 GTT Mark III Flex),

LNG SCHNEEWEISSCHEN (180,000 m3 GTT NO96) and PACIFIC BREEZE (182,000 m3 Moss), and in the

classification of the largest FSRU in service the BAUHINIA SPIRIT (EX-MOL FSRU CHALLENGER 263,000 m3

with 5 GTT NO96 tanks). Furthermore BV has been the first class involved in LNG carrier classification of a

200,000 m3 capacity with 4 tanks (fourteen units, three of them in service are included in the statistics of

this document). More information related to LNG carriers by cargo containment system in the range of

170,000 to 200,000 m3 cargo capacity is detailed in paragraph 2.3.1.

BV has considerable experience with gas fuelled engines and more specifically 4 stroke (DFDE) and 2 stroke (X-DF) propulsion, being the pioneers with the DFDE 75,000 m3 GLOBAL ENERGY delivered in 2006 and the X-DF 180,000 m SK AUDACE delivered in 2017. Since 2006, 55 LNG carriers with DFDE propulsion

have been built with Bv class. Bv has type approved the dual fuel engines proposed by the major

manufacturers in particular MAN, WARTSILA and HiMSEN usually installed on these types of ship. In

particular, BV is a leading classification society in the segment of X-DF LNG carriers with around 53 units

built or under construction equipped with this type of twin screw propulsion system. More recently our

classification society has been the first to work on the certification of the brand-new MAN B&W 6G70ME-

C10.5-GA for part of the Dynagas orders of 200,000 m3 capacity LNG carriers at HHI.

2.6 Experiences with alternative technologies

2.6.1 Air Lubrication System (ALS)

As mentioned in the previous paragraph in addition to new reliquefaction and subcooling systems, the most common technologies usually proposed in LNG carriers are the shaft generators (PTO) and air

lubrication systems (ALS).

57 modern 174,000 ~ 180,000 m3 LNG carriers in service are equipped with PTO technology and there are

98_on order some of them with Bv class. This may be an obvious proven technology to save fuel during

navigation considering the SFOC of the 2-stroke DF engines compared with the DF generator sets. Clutch

solutions can be proposed and potentially power take in (PTI) and hybrid propulsion for peak saving

and/or low speed and ship manouvring or use of alternative power units rather than internal combustion

engines to propell the LNG carriers.

ALS systems have been implemented also very recently in the LNG carrier fleet. There are currently 44

modern 174,000 ~ 180,000 m3 LNG carriers in service and 133 on order equipped with any of the available

systems, basically HANWHA OCEAN, HHI-Himsen, SHI-Saver air or Silverstream. From the operation point

of view the sea condition is an important parameter to be considered.
