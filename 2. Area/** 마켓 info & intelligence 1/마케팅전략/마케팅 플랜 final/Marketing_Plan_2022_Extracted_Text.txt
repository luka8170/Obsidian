works with our Clients on reducing the technical and operational risks. Our 
global network of experts is on hand 24/7 to support our clients staying

performance. We evolve continually with the industry, developing new Rules 
and tools to support innovation and increased performance.

Shipowners and  Shipmanagement companies registered in Hong Kong

Our vision  
Shaping a world of trust. We are dedicated to building trust between client companies, public authorities 
and consumers. 
 
Our mission  
 
Our mission is to reduce our clients’ risks, improve their performance and help them innovate to meet the 
challenges of quality, health & safety, environmental protection and social responsibility 
 
Products and services 
What we’re selling…… 
We are selling premium classification, statutory certification, material and equipment certification services 
and customized add-value technical solutions services to shipowners and shipmanagers for their business 
needs. 
 
Expected demand 
 
We are expecting our clients will rank BV as the trusted technical partner in classification services as to 
safeguard rules and regulations compliance of their fleet. 
Due to more and more stringent requirements in environmental protection for marine air and sea of our 
planet, the new regulatory frameworks will drive shipowners to operate their assets in a greener, safer, 
improved performance and efficiency in order to safeguard their business sustainability and development. 
We expect owner will renovate the fleet with new environmental friendly vessels such as LNG fuelled ship, 
diesel-electric hybrid, alternate clean fuels such as ammonia and hydrogen as well as the recovery of wind 
and solar energy in the propulsion system. The emergent of new innovative ideas will stimulate 
shipowners to embrace the change and fast moving forward to ensure they have the passport to operate.   
For such, we will promote our BV expertise and to demonstrate our strong technical supports to the 
clients, their fleets and management systems in order to meet their business needs and expectation on 
classification society services. Other than that, we will extend our scope to promote other value-added 
consultancy services from BVS such as cyber security, CAP, ESG compliance verification, ship performance, 
fuel efficiency, alternate and clean fuel solutions and so on.

The problem 
 
In an attempt to identify the market problems that we aim to solve for customers, and to understand client’s 
expectation on BV, we have run through the “Customer Satisfaction Survey” campaign in December 2021. 
Hypothesis results showed that BV will require to enhance actions in two service aspects linked to “sales 
support” and “technical communication” for which are adversely affected BV to compare with our competitors. 
In addition, over the 2 - 3 decades, we have not delegated commercial team in Hong Kong to take lead of the 
sales and marketing activities for Hong Kong Shipowner markets. In the old days, the sales and marketing was 
mainly driven by operation and service that rendered by operation team and Marine Manager and also by the  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
established P2P relationship between Country Chief Executive and the General Manager with shipowners. The 
outcome showed that the capability and effectiveness to acquire new shipowners and expand our client’s 
base was limited. However, as from Year 2014 onward, the S & M of Hong Kong Shipowners was in charge by 
BV Shanghai HQ S & M Director – Owner Market and in co-ordination with BV HK Marine Manager. 
Unfortunately, due to the weak link of marketing communication / interaction with shipowners was not able 
to penetrate the clients for business development due to weak presence in Hong Kong in the last 3 years as a 
matter of the COVID pandemic and also social unrest incident as well in this period.  
 
Our solution 
How our business solves the problems?  
To make BV a uniqueness organization and differentiate from the other competitive rivals will require a 
thorough strategic marketing plan with well-defined marketing strategies and tactics to envisage the 
challenges in such a turbulent business environment. This is how we’ll succeed in the market.

Marketing strategy 
Since classification service portfolio is different from other service sectors, traditional 4Ps marketing mix 
strategy i.e. Product, Place, Price and Promotion is not best fit. Instead, a modified 4Ps by mixing the 
traditional 4Ps elements and the extended 3Ps will be considered as better solution for the new marketing 
strategy and is presented in this marketing plan. 
Modified 4Ps Marketing Mix Strategy  
 
- Promotion 
- Price 
- Product 
- Process 
 
Which are elaborated more details as follows:- 
 
Promotion 
It is undoubtedly that promotion of BV by direct marketing and relationship marketing to Shipowners and 
managers is crucial. It follows that the Go-To-The-Market approach by scheduled marketing visits and 
meeting clients so as to bring BV expertise, recent innovative development, rules updates and added-value 
service to clients as well as to understand client needs is strategically essential. In addition, marketing 
communication sessions by means of periodical webinars, seminars, workshops and trainings will be deliver 
to the targeted clients in order to promote BV’s full engagement in shipping updates. Social media such as 
Linkedin will engage clients to the BV group marketing activities.  
 
To be more specific, the Go-To-The-Market strategy will enable us…. 
1. To see the customers and talk to them face to face as to understand their needs and expectation from 
BV while to cultivate B2B relationship. 
2. To collect business intelligence information and to obtain other business opportunities hence the 
competitors’ news and their tactical view on competitive marketplace through the customers 
3. To uphold BV technical service portfolio image, updating any new rules, regulations as well as the 
recent innovative technology and research development 
4. To promote of new BV services e.g.  cross-selling for BVS service portfolio 
5. To plan the courtesy visits of BV top management to the customers

Price 
Price is a sensitive factor in business environment especially in a unique and highly competitive oligopoly 
classification service market in Hong Kong for which shipowner will compare the price level among all 
classification societies rendering the same services. Service fee comparison with other competitors in the 
manner of block fee agreement should be in line. So far, based on the information of our OPS Team and 
Marine Manager, BV’s FYF level are more or less in line with our competitors LR, ABS, DNV and NK. On the 
other hand, in a hope to obtain CAC from other class, special consideration of discount to those potential 
customers as incentives to transfer the class.
