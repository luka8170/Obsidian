## 🔍 Key Findings & Insights

### 1. Survey Cycle Cost Patterns

**Special Survey Years (Peak Cost)**
- GLORY HANA 2020 (3rd SS): ₩17.5M ($11,966)
- NURI HANA 2021 (3rd SS): ₩13.4M ($9,195)
- HANA PIONEER 2020 (5th SS): ₩10.6M ($7,243)

**Annual & Intermediate Surveys (Regular Cost)**
- Annual surveys: ₩3.6~7.3M ($2,500~$5,000)
- Intermediate surveys: ₩7.9~11.2M ($5,400~$7,700)

➡️ **Special Survey costs are approximately 2-3x higher than regular surveys**

### 2. Cost Efficiency by Vessel Size

| Vessel | GT | Annual Avg (KRW) | Annual Avg (USD) | Cost/GT (KRW) | Cost/GT (USD) | Efficiency Rank |
|--------|-----|------------------|------------------|---------------|---------------|-----------------|
| NURI HANA | 1,863 | ₩7.35M | $5,027 | ₩3,944 | $2.70 | 1st (Best) |
| GLORY HANA | 1,930 | ₩9.60M | $6,569 | ₩5,077 | $3.47 | 2nd |
| HANA PIONEER | 887 | ₩6.03M | $4,123 | ₩6,795 | $4.65 | 3rd |

➡️ **Economy of Scale Effect**: Larger vessels show better cost efficiency per GT (age factor must be considered)

### 3. Age vs. Cost Correlation

- HANA PIONEER (26 years old): ₩6,795/GT ($4.65/GT) - Highest
- GLORY HANA / NURI HANA (20 years old): ₩3,944~5,077/GT ($2.70~$3.47/GT)

➡️ **Older vessels incur higher classification costs per GT**

### 4. Cost Breakdown Summary

**By Survey Type (Estimated Annual Distribution)**
- Special Surveys: ~40% of total costs (occurs every 5 years)
- Intermediate Surveys: ~35% of total costs
- Annual Surveys: ~25% of total costs
- CMS (Continuous Machinery Survey): Included in all surveys

## 📌 Data Notes & Assumptions

**Data Source**
- Hanamarin-provided classification cost data
- Analysis period: 2020-2024 actual survey costs

**Currency & Exchange Rate**
- Base currency: Korean Won (KRW)
- USD conversion rate: 1 USD = 1,462 KRW (as of February 10, 2026)
- Source: Federal Reserve H.10 Foreign Exchange Rates

**Costs Included**
- Special Surveys (periodic comprehensive inspections every 5 years)
- Annual Surveys (yearly inspections)
- Intermediate Surveys (mid-cycle inspections between special surveys)
- CMS (Continuous Machinery Survey) fees
- Standard class survey fees

**Costs NOT Included**
- Additional surveys (damage, modification, special requirements)
- Emergency repairs or unscheduled inspections
- Special certification fees (tonnage, loadline, etc.)
- Statutory survey fees (may be bundled or separate)
- Travel/accommodation costs for surveyors (if applicable)

**Assumptions**
- All vessels maintain KR class throughout the data period
- Costs represent normal operational survey cycles
- No major modifications or damage surveys included
- CMS program active on all vessels
