

### 🎯 **Step 1: Determine M&O EC Submission Requirements**

**Mandatory Submission Criteria (any one applies):**

- 💰 **€1M+ contracts** (Offshore: per year basis, Marine: per contract term)
- 🆕 **New products/services** with liability/warranty issues
- 🔒 **Unlimited parent company guarantee** requirements
- ⚡ **Beyond Zone VP delegation limits**

**Deal Breakers - Immediate Submission Required (regardless of value):**

- 🚫 Scope outside BV normal business (design work, property development, etc.)
- 📜 Work requiring BV-unqualified accreditation/certification
- ⚖️ Potential conflicts of interest (jeopardizing existing accreditations)
- 💸 High risk + unlimited liability or indirect damages responsibility
- 🌍 Transactions with sanctioned parties/countries

### 📝 **Step 2: Pre-submission Preparation (Complete 10+ days before)**

**Mandatory 3-Document Package:**

1. **📊 Case Summary Template**
    - Company presentation & proposal description
    - Risk identification (Local vs HO team comments)
    - Engagement Committee recommendations
2. **📈 Risk Scoring Sheet**
    - Project context assessment
    - Technical/contractual/financial scoring
    - Deal Breaker checklist
3. **🎯 Project Overview PPT**
    - Comprehensive project presentation

### 👥 **Step 3: Internal Review Process**

**Local M&O Manager Responsibilities:**

- ✅ Initiate process & coordinate information collection
- ✅ Multi-team collaboration review:
    - **Country Team** (local conditions)
    - **Finance Team** (financial review)
    - **Legal Team** (legal assessment)
- ✅ Comprehensive analysis: commercial, financial, technical, operational, legal, contractual aspects

**HO Review Requirements:**

- 🔍 **M&O General Counsel mandatory review** (all submissions)
- 🔍 Subject matter expert consultations

### 🏛️ **Step 4: M&O EC Composition & Meeting**

**Permanent Members (mandatory attendance):**

- BV M&O President
- Concerned M&O Zone Vice President
- BV M&O Finance Director
- BV M&O General Counsel

**Ad Hoc Members (as needed):**

- BV M&O Technical & Operations Senior VP
- BV M&O Compliance Officer
- Concerned M&O Country/Regional CEO
- Local Legal Counsel/Financial Controller

**Meeting Format:** Ad hoc basis (physical/phone/Teams/email)

### ⚖️ **Step 5: Go/No Go Decision Making**

**Assessment Criteria:**

- 📊 Risk scoring (High/Medium/Low)
- 🚨 Deal Breaker presence
- 💼 Contract terms adequacy
- 🎯 BV capabilities & experience alignment
- 💰 Profitability & financial stability

**Decision Outcomes:**

- ✅ **GO**: Contract approval granted
- ❌ **NO GO**: Contract termination
- ⚠️ **Conditional GO**: Proceed with specific conditions

### 🔒 **Step 6: Final Contract Execution**

**Signing Authority Verification:**

- ✅ Signatory authorized to represent BV entity
- ✅ Contract amount within signatory's authority limits
- ❌ Insufficient authority → Superior approval required

**Negotiation Re-consultation:**

- 🔄 Major condition changes → M&O EC re-consultation
- 🤝 Deal Breaker impasses → Guidance requests

### ⚠️ **Step 7: Critical Restrictions**

**Absolute Prohibitions:**

- ❌ Post-award contract submissions
- ❌ Non-compliance with 10-day advance submission rule
- ❌ Proceeding despite Deal Breaker presence

**Continuous Monitoring:**

- 🔍 Immediate reporting of negotiation condition changes
- 🔍 Immediate consultation for unexpected risk discoveries

### ✅ **Final Verification Checklist**

**Pre-Submission:**

- [ ] M&O EC submission criteria verification
- [ ] Three mandatory documents completed
- [ ] Internal review completed (local + legal teams)
- [ ] Minimum 10-day advance submission

**Post-Approval:**

- [ ] GO decision documentation filed
- [ ] Signing authority re-verification
- [ ] Final contract terms review
- [ ] Negotiation change monitoring

**Key Process Reminders:**

- 🕐 **Timeline**: Minimum 10 days before tender submission
- 🚫 **No Late Submissions**: EC will not review contracts already awarded
- 🔄 **Re-consultation Allowed**: During final negotiations for deal breaker impasses
- 🤐 **Confidentiality**: Limit meeting participation to key personnel
- 📋 **Documentation**: All submissions must include Risk Scoring Form and Case Summary

This systematic approach ensures BV risk minimization, compliance adherence, and secure contract execution.
