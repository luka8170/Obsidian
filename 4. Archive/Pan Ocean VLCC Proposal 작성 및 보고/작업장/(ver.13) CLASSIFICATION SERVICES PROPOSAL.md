## Proposed class notations

The following class notations are proposed for modern Containerships in the frame of this proposal based on the basic information provided by Sinokor.

### I HULL MACH

**Container ship Unrestricted Navigation**

**VeriSTAR-Hull CM, Spectral Fatigue (Worldwide, 25)**

**WhiSp2, LASHING-RSSA, EGCS-scrubber, LI-HG-S2, LI-LASHING AUT-UMS, ALP, MON-SHAFT, CYBER RESILIENT,**

**BWT, CLEANSHIP, CPS (WBT), GREEN PASSPORT, INWATERSURVEY, LNGFUEL-PREPARED**

The description of each class notation is the following:

|   |   |
|---|---|
|**Key Notation**|**Description**|
|**I**|(Class Symbol) is assigned to ships built in accordance with the requirements of the Bureau Veritas Rules and maintained in a condition considered satisfactory by the Society|
|**HULL**|Special survey at yard for hull construction by Bureau Veritas|
|**MACH**|Special survey at yard for machinery installations by Bureau Veritas|
|**Container ship**|Ship type and survey programme|
|**Unrestricted Navigation**|Operation in any area and any period of the year|
|**VeriSTAR-Hull CM**|Assigned to ships, the structural condition of which is checked with 3D FEM calculation program at design stage according to the requirements of the Society. A fatigue assessment is also carried out with hotspot mapping|
|**Spectral Fatigue (Worldwide, 25)**|The additional class notation may be assigned when the fatigue check of structural details is performed through spectral fatigue techniques. The information between brackets is a short description of routes and areas considered for this spectral fatigue analysis and associated design fatigue life|
|**WhiSp2**|Assigned to ships where hydro-structure calculations taking into account the springing and whipping structural responses are performed, and ultimate strength assessment takes into account the non-linear effects of whipping|
|**LASHING-RSSA**|Can be assigned to ships which may be engaged in voyages on specific routes or in specific areas. The specific routes and specific areas are to be indicated between brackets (e.g.:<br><br>LASHING-RSSA(Asia-Europe through Mediterranean sea; Baltic sea))|
|**EGCS-scrubber**|Assigned to ships fitted with an Exhaust Gas Cleaning System using scrubber(s)|
|**LI-HG-S2**|Ship equipped with a loading instrument performing hull girder strength calculations, intact stability calculations and damage stability on a basis of a limit curve|
|**LI-LASHING**|Ship equipped with loading instrument performing lashing calculations|

|   |   |
|---|---|
|**AUT-UMS**|Ship fitted with automated installations enabling machinery spaces to remain periodically unattended in all sailing conditions including manoeuvring|
|**ALP**|Ship for lifting appliances intended to be used in harbours or similarly sheltered areas|
|**MON-SHAFT**|Ship fitted with a temperature monitoring system for the tailshaft stern tube aft bearing. The assignment of this notation allows the ship to be granted a reduced scope for complete tailshaft surveys|
|**CYBER RESILIENT**|Covers cyber security controls which are based in particular on the management of administration, and engineering specifications subject to the requirements for procedures, software and hardware secure design|
|**BWT**|Assigned to ships installed with ballast water treatment systems|
|**CLEANSHIP**|Ship so designed and equipped as to control and limit the emission of polluting substances in the sea and the air|
|**CPS (WBT)**|Ship complying with the requirements of Coating Performance Standard (PSPC). See NR530|
|**GREEN PASSPORT**|Ship for which requirements intended to facilitate ship recycling have been applied, encompassing the identification, quantification and localization of materials which may cause harm to the environment and people when the fittings or equipment containing such materials are removed, or when the ship is recycled. See NR528|
|**INWATERSURVE Y**|May be assigned to ships provided with suitable arrangements to facilitate the in-water surveys|
|**LNGFUEL-PREP ARED**|May be assigned to new ships that are designed to accommodate future installation of an LNG fuel system|
