## Key Market Insights (2021-2025)

### Bureau Veritas Strategic Lessons
1. **2022 Success Model**: BV+KR dual classification in LNG carriers = 31.6% market share (1,497,437 GT)
2. **LNG Market Specialization**: BV's technical expertise in gas carriers highly valued
3. **International Reach**: Effective use of Japanese, Turkish, Singapore shipyards
4. **Market Volatility**: Korean market highly cyclical - BV needs flexible strategy


## Conclusion

Bureau Veritas achieved peak performance in 2022 by successfully positioning itself as the preferred international classification society for dual classification with Korean Register, particularly in the LNG carrier market (1,246,329 GT across 11 vessels). The dramatic market volatility and classification data quality issues of 2023-2025 highlight the need for sustainable, technically-focused strategies. The dual classification approach provides the most promising path for future market penetration, as evidenced by the 2022 success where BV secured over 1.2M GT through strategic positioning.
