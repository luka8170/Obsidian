## 4. Strategic Response

### Option A: Status Quo

**Pros:** Protect profitability standards, avoid price war  
**Cons:** 
- ❌ Certain loss of $510,000 annual revenue opportunity
- ❌ Loss of 4.8M GT
- ❌ Strengthens KR market dominance
- ❌ Damages relationship

**Expected Outcome:** All 30 vessels go to KR

### Option B: Strategic Price Adjustment (Recommended)

**Proposal:**
- **$16,500/year** (3% reduction from current $17,000)
- Single rate for all 10+ year VLCCs
- **Provisional** - adjustable after KR pricing verification

**Rationale:**
1. **Minimal Risk:** Only $500 per vessel reduction
2. **Volume Compensation:** 30 vessels × $16,500 = $495,000/year (vs. current $136,000, +264%)
3. **Commitment Signal:** Addresses Sinokor's core need - "demonstrating competitive will"
4. **Strategic Value:** 4.8M GT + long-term relationship investment

**Cost-Benefit Analysis:**

| Current | 8 | $136,000 | Baseline |
