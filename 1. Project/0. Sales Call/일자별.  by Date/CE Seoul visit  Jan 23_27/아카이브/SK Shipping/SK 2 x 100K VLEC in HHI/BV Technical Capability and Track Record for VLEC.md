## 1. BV Technical Capability for VLEC (Very Large Ethane Carrier)

### 1.1 VLEC Technical Expertise

Bureau Veritas possesses comprehensive technical capabilities required for VLEC (Very Large Ethane Carrier) design and construction:

**Ethane Transportation Expertise:**

- **Temperature Management**: Design review capability for cargo systems at ethane transport temperature of -89℃
- **Pressure Control**: Tank pressure design approval considering ethane's saturated vapor pressure characteristics
- **Physical Properties**: System optimization based on ethane's density, viscosity, thermal conductivity and other physical properties

**VLEC Specialized System Technology:**

- **100,000㎥ Large-scale Design**: Design review experience for large-scale VLEC including MARIC design
- **Type C Tank System**: Design approval for large independent pressure vessel systems (currently 5 vessels under construction in China)
- **Membrane System**: AiP (Approval in Principle) completed for GTT Slim system and other advanced membrane technologies for VLEC
- **Refrigeration System**: Verification of ethane-dedicated refrigeration cycle and re-liquefaction systems
- **Safety System**: Gas detection, emergency shutdown, and inerting systems tailored to ethane characteristics
