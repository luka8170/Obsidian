## **Proposed/optional class notations**

### Proposed class notations

The following selected class notations are proposed for this project:

**I, ✠HULL, ✠MACH, Oil tanker, CSR, CPS(WBT), ESP, Tier III, Unrestricted navigation, ✠VeriSTAR-HULL CM, ✠AUT-UMS, XSYS-NEQ-1, MON-SHAFT, CPS(COT), BWE, BWT, INWATERSURVEY, CLEANSHIP, VCS, SPM, GREEN PASSPORT, EGCS-SCRUBBER, LI-HG-S3, LNGFUEL-PREPARED, METHANOLFUEL-PREPARED, ERS-S, ESA**

The description of each class notation is the following:

**I:** (Class Symbol) is assigned to ships built in accordance with the requirements of the BV Rules and maintained in a condition considered satisfactory by the Society.

**✠HULL:** special survey at yard for hull construction by BV.

**✠MACH:** special survey at yard for machinery installations by BV.

**Oil tanker**: service notation for sea going self-propelled ships which are constructed generally with integral tanks and intended primarily to carry in bulk crude oil, other oil products, or oil-like substances having any flash point.

**CSR**: IACS Common Structural Rules Harmonized (2015 and further amendments).

**CPS (WBT):** ship complying with the requirements of Coating Performance Standard (PSPC), ballast tanks.

**ESP**: Enhanced survey programme.

**Tier III**: assigned to ships for which the main engine and auxiliary engines are issued with an EIAPP certificate (Engine International Air Pollution Prevention Certificate) stating compliance with Tier III according to the applicable MARPOL Convention requirements.

**Unrestricted navigation:** operation in any area and any period of the year.

**✠VeriSTAR-Hull CM** ship the structural condition of which is checked with 3D FEM calculation program at design stage according to the requirements of the Society. A fatigue assessment has been carried out on selected structural details showing that their evaluated design fatigue life is not less than 25 years. A hot spot map has been made available for construction surveys and is kept on board the ship after delivery.

**✠AUT-UMS:** ship fitted with automated installations enabling machinery spaces to remain periodically unattended in all sailing conditions including manoeuvring.

**XSYS-NEQ-1:** ship fitted with a centralized navigation control system so laid out and arranged that it enables normal navigation and manoeuvring operation of the ship by one person, for periodical one man on watch.

**MON-SHAFT:** ship fitted with a temperature monitoring system for the tailshaft sterntube aft bearing. The assignment of this notation allows the ship to be granted a reduced scope for complete tailshaft surveys.

**CPS (COT):** ship complying with the requirements of Coating Performance Standard (PSPC), cargo tanks.

**BWE:** Ship intended for ballast water exchange at sea and whose design is in compliance with the technical provisions of BWM convention (2004), Regulation D-1.

**BWT:** ship fitted with a ballast water treatment plant. See NI.538.

**INWATERSURVEY:** ship provided with suitable arrangements to facilitate the in-water surveys.

**CLEANSHIP:** ship so designed and equipped as to control and limit the emission of polluting substances in the sea and the air.

**VCS:** for ships equipped with cargo vapour control systems.

**SPM:** ship fitted with a single point mooring installation.

**GREEN PASSPORT:** ship for which requirements intended to facilitate ship recycling have been applied, For EU flagged vessels the **GREEN PASSPORT EU** notation is proposed to cover all requirements for IHM Certificate according to EU Ship Recycling Regulation.

**EGCS-SCRUBBER**: ship fitted with an Exhaust Gas Cleaning System using Scrubber(s)

**LI-HG-S3**: ship equipped with a loading instrument performing; hull girder calculations and intact stability calculations and direct damage stability calculations based on pre-programmed damage cases.

**LNGFUEL-PREPARED:** ships that are designed with specific arrangements to accommodate future installation of an LNG fuel gas system.

**METHANOLFUEL-PREPARED:** Ships designed to accommodate future installation of a methanol fuel system.

**ERS-S:** Provision of technical assistance in case of a maritime accident at sea by providing information on their remaining strength and stability in the resulting damaged condition.

**ESA:** compliance of the ship propulsion shaft lines with NR592 “Elastic Shaft Alignment”. Stern bearings machining and alignment, as well as other bearing offsets, are to be optimized in order to reach the most favourable load distribution for relevant operating conditions. Prime mover or gearbox is to be positioned to get acceptable loads on each support, and to anticipate thermal expansion and hull deformation effects.

## **Optional class notations**

### **5.2.1 Cyber Security**

Today’s ships are increasingly using connected systems to improve operations and business. In particular, monitoring and remote management systems are becoming a more integral feature in improving ship processes and overall efficiency. Bureau Veritas collaborates with marine stakeholders to develop rules and notations that provide a comprehensive cybersecurity framework. Covering organizational and technical measures, we help ship owners protect their assets, and we help shipyards and manufacturers comply with IMO and IACS cyber requirements.

For this project the following two notations are proposed (one of the two to apply):

**CYBER RESILIENT:** Addressing Cyber Risk by network design

**CYBER MANAGED:** Addressing cyber risk by management system and policy

In more detail:

**CYBER RESILIENT:** This notation ensures compliance with IACS UR E26 “cyber resilience of ships,” which enters into force on July 1, 2024, and will make cybersecurity a mandatory topic to be addressed by Yards.

Application of this notation will ensure that the vessel’s network architecture is so designed and arranged as to reduce cyber risk. The vessels will be certified against an upcoming regulation. To apply this notation cooperation between the shipyard, owners and BV is required to ensure that the network design conforms with the requirements.

**CYBER MANAGED:** This notation enables shipowners to develop and implement a cyber risk management system using a risk-based methodology and standardized framework. It relies on a complete map of their IT and OT systems, describing among other connections with shore, risk analysis, high-level management policies and detailed onboard procedures. Application of this notation does not necessarily require changes in vessel design or network architecture.

- Sewage sludges handling and storage for longer periods
- Oil spill kits
- Hydrocarbon detectors in cooling systems
- Use of refrigerants with reduced GWP and zero ODP

**GWT:** The additional class notation GWT is assigned to ships fitted with a treatment installation for grey waters.
