**Subject:** SINOKOR's TOC to KR Class and FYF Competitiveness Review

Dear Mr. Pinteric

We would like to report that SINOKOR, which transferred its vessels acquired in 2023 to BV, has decided all eight VLCCs acquired in 2024 with KR following a significant reduction in KR’s FYF for SINOKOR in early 2024.

Additionally, even vessel previously under KR & BV double class, ‘Gulf Sunrise’ is now being adjusted to KR single class.

This situation necessitates a comprehensive review of the competitiveness of our FYF offerings for SINOKOR. So, we have been currently in close discussions with SINOKOR regarding this matter.

**1. Situation Overview**

**![](cid:image013.png@01DAFABE.28CF5EA0)**

SINOKOR has been actively acquiring VLCCs since 2023.

In 2023, SINOKOR transferred the class of **three purchased VLCCs to BV**.     

([Riviera - News Content Hub - VLCC sales lead to a mix of tankers changing hands (rivieramm.com)](https://www.rivieramm.com/news-content-hub/news-content-hub/vlcc-sales-lead-a-mixture-of-tankers-changing-hands-77169)

However, following a visit from **KR Chairman Mr. HC Lee** **in early 2024,** during which KR **significantly lowered its FYF** for all vessel types,

SINOKOR decided to **transferred the class of 8 VLCCs** (**5 from Frontline** and **3 from Euronav**) acquired in 2024 **to KR instead of BV**.

[Frontline sells non-eco ships to have entire fleet fuel-efficient - Offshore Energy (offshore-energy.biz)](https://www.offshore-energy.biz/frontline-sells-non-eco-ships-to-have-entire-fleet-fuel-efficient/)

[Sinokor takes Euronav VLCCs - Splash247](https://splash247.com/sinokor-takes-euronav-vlccs/)

According to SINOKOR's senior management, the adjustment in **KR’s FYF has made BV’s FYF considerably higher in comparison**.

**2. Current Profitability of SINOKOR's VLCCs**

The overall profitability of SINOKOR’s VLCC as below.

![](cid:image014.png@01DAFABE.28CF5EA0)

**3. Outlook**

SINOKOR is likely to continue purchasing second-hand vessels, including VLCCs continuously.

However, it is expected that these vessels will continue to be classified under KR, given the current trend and FYF advantages offered by KR.

**4. Considering points**

To encourage SINOKOR to consider transferring their future vessel acquisitions to BV classification, a reassessment of the current FYF structure on a per-vessel basis is required. This may involve adjusting BV’s FYF to be more competitive with KR.

**5. Current Discussions with SINOKOR**

While SINOKOR has indicated that they cannot disclose the exact FYF levels offered by KR**, they have expressed willingness to provide a comparative indication if BV's FYF is adjusted and shared with them**.

SINOKOR can then inform us whether the revised FYF is higher or lower compared to KR.

This feedback would enable us to make minimal yet strategic adjustments to our FYF, making it more appealing for SINOKOR to consider transferring new vessels to BV class.
