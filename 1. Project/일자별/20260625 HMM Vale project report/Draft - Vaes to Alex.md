Dear Alex,

Thank you for sharing the TradeWinds alert. To answer your questions directly:

**Yes, this project is on our radar.** BV Korea and BV China have been tracking it jointly. However, we did not include it in the June 8th report because at that time the shipyard selection was unresolved and, more importantly, HMM is a state-owned enterprise effectively controlled by Korea Development Bank (KDB). As a result, HMM is subject to annual parliamentary audits and is structurally bound to select KR as their default class for newbuildings — selecting a foreign class requires formal justification, and failure to satisfy auditors risks serious administrative and political consequences for HMM management. We therefore did not consider this a high-probability BV pipeline.

**Sangjin has been actively engaged on this topic**, pursuing a two-track strategy:

**1. Long-term HMM cultivation:** We are building a concrete BV track record with HMM via the Zhejiang Tenglong Shipyard project (TL 2418/TL 2420), currently under BV class. HMM's team visited the shipyard this week; the positive signal is that they are likely to retain BV class rather than transfer to KR — a meaningful precedent given their standard KR-only policy. We are supporting them on the Loading Computer approval and photo documentation in BV survey reports to strengthen this relationship further. We also held a senior-level meeting with HMM Ocean Service CEO and Head of Marine Management last month, with a joint technical follow-up visit (with our FST team) planned for July.

**2. Immediate focus on Polaris Shipping:** As HMM faces political constraints, we are concentrating our near-term efforts on Polaris Shipping, which has a Vale COA and is actively finalizing a 2 firm + 2 option Newcastlemax order with no class restriction. Two days ago (June 23), Sangjin and I met personally with Mr. HS Han, Chairman of Polaris. Chairman Han described himself as a "Big Fan of BV" and expressed strong commitment to supporting BV in their upcoming newbuilding program — he was the person who personally selected BV for Polaris's feeder container project at Huanghai Shipyard. Polaris has strong BV references (Capesize/Newcastlemax BCs at SWS, 1,800 TEU feeders at Huanghai), and we have been coordinating with Huarong Financial Leasing and Nantong Xiangyu Shipyard, which already has 2×210K DWT Newcastlemax BCs of SDARI design in their orderbook and can offer late 2029 delivery slots. BV Korea and BV China are fully aligned on this campaign.

We are confident in the Polaris opportunity and will keep you closely updated.
