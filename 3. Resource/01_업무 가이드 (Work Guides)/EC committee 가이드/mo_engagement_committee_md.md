# M&O Engagement Committee Rules

February 2021

## 1. PURPOSE

In the framework of a growing and more complex organization, the BV M&O Management has
decided to reinforce and formalize the validation process for large and risky contracts.

It was decided in April 2017 to create an M&O Engagement Committee (the "M&O EC").

The M&O EC has the following objectives:

- Define a process to review and validate the commercial, financial, legal and insurance-
related aspects of M&O significant commercial offers, projects, contracts and
undertakings;
- Formalize a "Go / No Go" decision.

## 2. SCOPE

Any commercial offer or project or contract meeting any one of the following criteria must be
submitted to the M&O EC:

### 1) All offers that must be submitted to the M&O EC pursuant to BV M&O Approval Matrix, as reproduced below:

- Clients offers & contracts exceeding the monetary amounts of 1 MEUR (per
year for Offshore Contract and per term for Marine Contracts);
- Offers involving the provision of new products or services with related
liabilities/warranties issues; or
- Offers that require an unlimited parent company guarantee; or
- Any offer not satisfying the above criteria but that BV M&O management wants
to submit to the M&O EC due to the level of assessed risks.

### 2) Offers containing the following Group Deal Breakers, regardless of value of offers/contracts:

- Scope of work outside BV normal course of business (including new service) and/or
involving uninsured activities; or
- Scope of work requiring an accreditation/qualification that BV does not hold; or
- Potential conflict of interest (e.g. scope of work
jeopardizing an accreditation/qualification/certification held); or
- Offers qualified as 'High' in the risk scoring where the BV M&O legal entity is
required to: (i) bear unlimited liabilities (no liability cap); or (ii) to be responsible for
indirect damages (no exclusion of liability for consequential loss); or
- Transactions involving sanctioned parties or countries (international trade
sanctions/embargoes/restrictions)

### 3) Any other offer that M&O management wishes to submit to the M&O EC due to the level of assessed risks.

## 3. EFFECTIVE DATE

The M&O EC started to operate as of July 1st, 2017.

## 4. MEMBERS

### Permanent members:

- BV M&O President
- Concerned M&O Zone Vice President
- BV M&O Finance Director
- BV M&O General Counsel

### Ad hoc members (depending on needs):

- BV M&O Technical & Operations Senior Vice President
- BV M&O Compliance Officer
- Concerned M&O Country Chief Executive or Regional Chief Executive
- Concerned local Legal Counsel or Financial Controller.

## 5. PROCESS

- It is the responsibility of local M&O manager and any relevant person in charge of offer
/ project / contract management to submit all offers meeting the abovementioned criteria
to the M&O EC.

- The local M&O manager shall initiate the process, collect all necessary information,
and review the offers (commercial, financial, technical, operational, legal and
contractual aspects) with their teams (country team, Finance and Legal).

- A summary of the key aspects of the project shall be prepared (including the Risk
Scoring Form and Case Summary/Template Form carefully and systematically
completed as attached hereunder) and submitted to the M&O EC as soon as
possible and at any rate at least 10 days prior to the tender submission date (if
any).

- M&O EC will not review late submissions relating to contracts already awarded or
offers already submitted. However, after an offer is submitted with green light from M&O
EC, the local M&O manager, during its final negotiations with the client, is allowed to
get back to the M&O EC to seek guidance when an impasse is reached on a deal
breaker item.

- The meetings of the M&O EC are on ad hoc basis (physical, phone or teams conference
or exchange of emails) to formalize "Go/ No Go" decision.

- All parties are encouraged to limit the participation to M&O EC meetings/conference to
key people to ensure efficiency of the process and maintain high level of confidentiality.

- All offers that must be submitted to the M&O EC shall be reviewed by the M&O General
Counsel.

- The finalization and collection of all relevant information and documents for submission
to the M&O Engagement Committee shall be done by the relevant local M&O manager.
