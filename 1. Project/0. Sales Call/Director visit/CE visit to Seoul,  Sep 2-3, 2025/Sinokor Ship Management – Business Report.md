## 3. Past Projects We Have Lost (and Reasons)

- **Shipyard**: Hyundai Heavy Industries<br>
- **Status**: Awarded to competitor society<br>
- **Impact**: Missed opportunity due to the strong KR chairman’s lobby<br><br>

### VLCC Acquisitions (2024) – Critical CAC Opportunity Lost  
- **Belgian Euronav** → 3 VLCCs (_Nectar, Newton, Noble_) ≈ USD 155M<br>
- **Norwegian Frontline** → 5 VLCCs (2009–2010 built) ≈ USD 290M<br>
- **Sinokor’s Willingness**: Transfer all 8 VLCCs to BV class<br>
- **Request**: Only symbolic FYF discount as goodwill<br>
- **BV Response**: Refusal (per CE directive)<br>
- **Consequence**: Loss of 8-vessel CAC project + severe relationship damage<br><br>

### Key Reasons for Losses  
1. Commercial inflexibility – especially FYF policy<br>
2. Weakness in large vessel segment competitiveness<br>
3. Aggressive competition from KR and others<br>
4. Relationship deterioration – Sinokor disappointed, now reluctant to meet BV
