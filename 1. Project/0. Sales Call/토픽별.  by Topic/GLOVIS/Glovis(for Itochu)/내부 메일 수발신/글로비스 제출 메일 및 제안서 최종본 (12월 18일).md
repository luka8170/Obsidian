Dear Hyundai Glovis Team,

First of all, please accept our sincere apologies for the one-day delay in submitting our proposal. We appreciate your understanding.

We are deeply grateful for the opportunity to present our classification services proposal for this important 174K LNGC project. It is truly an honor for Bureau Veritas to be considered as your potential classification partner for this vessel to be built at HD Hyundai Samho Heavy Industries for ITOCHU charter.

Please find our comprehensive proposal attached.

### Our Commitment

Bureau Veritas wants this project with Hyundai Glovis and we will provide high standards services throughout the vessel's lifecycle. Our attached proposal demonstrates our technical expertise, proven LNG carrier experience, and competitive offering.

### Commercial Terms

As detailed in the attached proposal, our Navigation Service (NS) fee structure for this single vessel is:

- **1st Term (Years 1-5): Free**
- **2nd Term (Years 6-10): USD 4,000 per year**
- **3rd Term (Years 11-15): USD 8,000 per year**

Because this is only one vessel, we cannot offer better terms at this time. However, this represents our strong commitment to establishing a partnership with Hyundai Glovis.

### Future Fleet Opportunity

We understand you may be considering additional vessel orders (1+1 vessels) next year. Should you order those vessels and decide to go with Bureau Veritas as well, then the picture will change. 

We will reconsider the fee structure for the period **after the first 5 years**, considering a batch of 3 vessels in-service instead of one. This would enable significantly more favorable terms.

### Next Steps

We would welcome the opportunity to discuss this proposal in detail. Please let us know your convenient schedule for a meeting.

Once again, thank you very much for this valuable opportunity. We look forward to the possibility of working together with Hyundai Glovis.
