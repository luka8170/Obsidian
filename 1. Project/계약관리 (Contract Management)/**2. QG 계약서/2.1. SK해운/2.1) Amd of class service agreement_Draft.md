**WHEREAS** the Parties agreed to amend the Agreement with regards to the applicable commercial terms as well as the scope of work of the Agreement.

**ARTICLE 1: PURPOSE**

- 1. The purpose of this Amendment is to modify the Agreement for the following terms:
        1. Modify ’1. Discount on Services’ mentioned under the Appendix 2 of the Agreement by deleting the NC surveyor secondment services element and modifying ’additional services’ offered by BV KOREA.
        2. Delete ‘3. Special rates’ and ‘4. Class rates’ in Appendix 2
        3. Modify the Additional Commercial Terms mentioned under the Appendix 3 of the Agreement by deleting references to future pricing offer by BV KOREA.

1.2 The present Amendment shall be effective from 4th of October 2023 (the “**Effective Date**”).

**ARTICLE 2: MODIFICATION OF THE ARTICLE 4 OF THE AGREEMENT**

2.1 The article 4.1 of the Agreement shall be modified as follow:

4.1 In consideration of the Services, the COMPANY shall pay BV the remuneration (the “PRICE”) as per Appendix 2. In addition to the PRICE the COMPANY shall benefit from the discount system described in in the Appendix 2 of the present Agreement.
