A detailed analysis of the factors affecting work to date reveals:

- The initial design submissions received numerous comments. Out of 227 reviewed drawings, 1930 comments were issued, averaging more than 8 comments per drawing, which is highly unusual. Significant effort and resources have been dedicated by BV to clarify and explain requirements in order to ensure compliance with applicable rules and to achieve a satisfactory level of safety and integrity.
- Despite large efforts from both sides, comments have been challenging to resolve as proposed solutions were not clearly compliant with the rules, leading to further discussions and postponements.
- The SRTP principles training sessions provided by BV during the early stages were not fully followed, resulting in additional discussions. The SRTP concept has been submitted four times, with the latest revision earlier this month. Huge progress has been made in this area.

Regarding BV's commitment, it can be reported that 3,366 man-hours have been spent on hull structure so far, a 240% overrun from original estimations for the project of this size. This underscores BV's full and unreserved dedication to the project and the Shipyard as our contractual client.

**Actions**:

- To further demonstrate BV's commitment and effort, BV DA Project Manager Roberto Spolaore has confirmed his plans to arrive in Korea on 3rd June, 2024, to work with DaeSun and KPO in resolving the remaining challenges smoothly.
- BV Korea (KPO and NC) have assigned additional PMs who are actively assisting the DaeSun shipyard design team, especially with the SRTP requirements.
- This week, DA is focusing on clearing the maximum possible number of comments.

- Comments that have already been agreed upon, and for which DA is waiting for new drawing revisions from SY.
- Comments related to zone construction drawings will be prioritized and addressed by the end of this week.
- The majority of comments associated with other drawings are planned to be answered by the end of this week.
- Comments related to fine mesh details will be pending the results of Fine Mesh Analysis.

I hope this response meets your satisfaction and demonstrates BV's commitment to this important project for DaeSun and Panstar. I also want to personally thank you for your continued support and trust in BV to deliver the best possible product for DaeSun and Panstar.
