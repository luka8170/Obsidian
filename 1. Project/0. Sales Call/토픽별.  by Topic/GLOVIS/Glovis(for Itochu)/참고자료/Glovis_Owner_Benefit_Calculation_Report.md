## 1. CONFIRMED TARIFF-BASED BENEFITS

### 1.1 Block Fee Structure (15 Years)
**Status:** ✅ **CONFIRMED - Contractual Commitment**  
**Source:** Worksheet for QG 2nd batch for Glovis.md - "Block Fee for 15 years"  
**Date:** Included in April 5, 2024 proposal

| Term | Duration | Annual Fee | Term Total | Market Rate* | Savings |
|------|----------|------------|------------|--------------|---------|
| **Term 1** | Years 1-5 | **FREE** | **$0** | $8,000/year | **$40,000** |
| **Term 2** | Years 6-10 | **$4,000** | **$20,000** | $8,000/year | **$20,000** |
| **Term 3** | Years 11-15 | **$8,000** | **$40,000** | $8,000/year | **$0** |

**Total Block Fee Cost:** $60,000 per vessel  
**Total Market Value:** $120,000 per vessel (estimated)  
**Net Savings:** **$60,000 per vessel**  
**Fleet Savings (2 vessels):** **$120,000**

*Market rate estimated based on typical classification society annual fees for Glovis

**What's Included in Block Fee:**
- All listed surveys (as per attachment)
- Class Dry Dock fees (explicitly confirmed)

**Calculation Detail:**
```
Term 1 Savings: 5 years × $8,000 = $40,000 saved
Term 2 Discount: 5 years × ($8,000 - $4,000) = $20,000 saved
Term 3 Market Rate: 5 years × $8,000 = $0 additional savings

Total 15-Year Block Fee Benefit: $60,000 per vessel
Fleet Total (2 vessels): $60,000 × 2 = $120,000
```

## 4. KEY ASSUMPTIONS AND NOTES

### 4.1 Assumptions

1. **Market Rate for Block Fees:** $8,000/year based on typical classification society annual fees for Glovis
2. **Service Value Estimation:** Conservative approach using lower market rates
3. **Construction Period:** 24-30 months
4. **Operational Period:** 15 years for in-service benefits
5. **Exchange Rates:** All values in USD
6. **Inflation:** Not adjusted for inflation

### 4.2 Important Notes

1. **Confirmed vs. Estimated:**
   - Tariff benefits ($120,000 for 2 vessels) are contractually confirmed
   - Service values ($1,276,000 - $2,010,000 for 2 vessels) are market-based estimates

2. **Actual Proposals Only:**
   - Includes ONLY services explicitly offered in April 5 and May 24, 2024 proposals
   - General BV capabilities not specifically offered are excluded

3. **Glovis Fleet:**
   - Total JV project: 4 vessels
   - Glovis share: 2 vessels
   - All calculations are for Glovis's 2 vessels

4. **EMSA Regulation Compliance:**
   - BV Solutions (BVS) cannot provide PMC activities for BV-classed vessels per EU/EC regulations
   - HSE support provided through BV classification team
