Regarding the subject matter, SIRE 2.0 requires verification of whether crew members are familiar with the method of estimating a vessel’s position during drift. We have also been informed that this has been raised as an observation under TMSA.

In this regard, we would like to inquire whether your Emergency Response Service (ERS) can assist us in determining drift estimation in emergency situations onboard.

**Additionally, we would like to inquire whether your service includes support for oil spill trajectory prediction in the event of an accidental discharge. We understand that this has recently become a point of concern under both DRY BMS and TMSA audits.**

If such services are not available under the current ERS subscription, please let me know whether a separate service is offered, along with relevant information on enrollment and annual maintenance fees.
