Dear Mr. Vaes,

Thank you for your inquiry regarding the owner's benefit we offered to Glovis for the Qatar Gas 2nd Batch project.

Before describing the Owner's Benefit (OB) we provided, I would like to first mention that QG 2nd Batch was structured as a Joint Venture between Glovis and K-Line, which required us to approach our proposal somewhat differently from typical projects.

**PROJECT BACKGROUND - K-LINE & GLOVIS JOINT VENTURE**

The Qatar Gas 2nd Batch had a unique Joint Venture structure with the following key features:

Project Profile:

- Owner: K-Line & Glovis Joint Venture

- Shipyard: Hanwha Ocean (formerly DSME)

- Operator: G-Marine Service & K-Line Singapore

- Flag: Singapore

- Total Award: 174K LNGC / 4 vessels ( 2 BV for Glovis + 2 DNV for K-Line)

Important Project Characteristics:

- Technical Leadership by K-Line: Due to K-Line's extensive experience in LNG carrier operations, they led all technical discussions and coordination for the JV

- JV Coordination: All technical matters and owner benefit requests were coordinated through K-Line as the consortium's technical lead

- Tender Process: The proposal was prepared and submitted to K-Line, who then coordinated with Glovis on behalf of the JV

**OWNER'S BENEFIT PACKAGE OFFERED**

- **Initial Main Proposal (on April 5th, 2024)**

We submitted a comprehensive proposal covering the following services and benefits:

1. Five-Year Block Fee Structure:

- TERM 1 (Years 1-5): FREE OF CHARGE

- TERM 2 (Years 6-10): USD 4,000/year (USD 20,000 for 5 years)

- TERM 3 (Years 11-15): USD 8,000/year (USD 40,000 for 5 years)

- Class Dry Dock fees INCLUDED in above block fee offer

- Covers all relevant surveys listed in the attached schedule

2. Premium Project Management (Complimentary):

- Dedicated Premium Project Managers:

  * NC Survey: Mr. Efstratios STAVROTHANASIS 

  * NC Approval: Mr. Yun-Je CHO 

- Three-tier organization structure (Executive / Monitoring / Delivery levels)

- Gas Expertise Team support from BV Head Office

- Close collaboration with BV Japan for comprehensive stakeholder support

- Support coordination in Korea, Japan, and Qatar throughout construction

3. Digital Tools and Communication (Free of Charge):

- VeriSTAR Project Management (VPM) platform access

- Regular weekly construction progress meetings

- Real-time information sharing on survey progress and design approval status

4. Pre-Delivery Services (Complimentary):

- Pre-delivery mock PSC inspection by experienced Ship in Service surveyors

- BV "PSC Ready" mobile application access

- Continuous PSC performance monitoring and scoring analysis

- Mock-up PSC checks during each ship survey

5. Emergency Response Service - ERS (Complimentary):

- 24/7 emergency response service available worldwide

- Immediate mobilization of qualified surveyors and engineers

- Online support from Head Office specialists for urgent repairs

- Dedicated support for cargo handling system and cargo containment system issues

- Privileged communication channel with owner and GTT for gas leakage incidents

6. Training Programs (Free of Charge):

Standard 5-day training course covering:

- Hull structure, Independent tanks, Membrane tanks

- Cargo Handling Systems

- Electricity, instrumentation, and hazardous area equipment

- Safety systems and Gas fuel systems

- 20+ HSE topics (PPE, confined space, working at height, etc.)

7. Technical Support Services (Available at Premium Project Discounted Rate):

- FAT attendance as Owner's representative (50% premium project discounted daily rate)

- BVS HSSE Supervision support (discounted premium project rate)

- Risk Assessment for Increasing Cargo Filling Limit to 98.5% (premium project rate)

- BVS online training for Crew and Fleet Management (discounted rate)

8. Additional Support (Complimentary):

- Dedicated Korean Key Account Manager

- Regular stakeholder meetings and transparent updates

- Problem solving and mediation support

- Technical presentations upon client request

- **HSE Support Package (on May 24th, 2024)**

After Glovis specifically requested HSE supervisor dispatch during construction, we proposed the following comprehensive HSE support at NO EXTRA COST:

1. HSE Support During Construction:

- HSSE Coordinators and HSSE Manager from our site team to collaborate with and assist the owner's HSE manager

- Support to comply with charterer's HSE requirements

- Implementation according to BV's utmost HSE standards

2. QHSE & Operation Director's Regular Support:

- Regular site visits by our QHSE & Operation Director (covering North Asia Zone)

- HSE training sessions as required by the owner

- On-site HSE inspections

Regulatory Context:

We clarified that due to EMSA regulations and BV M&O internal processes, BVS (BV Solutions) was strictly prohibited from engaging in PMC activities for BV classed vessels (this EU regulation applies uniformly to all EU ROs). Therefore, the HSE support was structured to be provided by our NC department's HSSE personnel.

- **Implementation Coordination (on September-October 2024)**

- Glovis inquired about deploying HSSE Managers starting from March 2025

- Coordination was conducted with K-Line as the consortium's technical lead

- Internal arrangements were discussed between BVS Korea and BV Class regarding HSE resource provision

- Contract arrangements were to be structured as: Kline/Glovis consortium with BVS directly for resources, and BVS with BV C&S on back charge fees

Given the successful support framework established in QG 2nd Batch, I recommend Mr. Kim's NC profitability analysis include a comparable owner's benefit package, particularly the HSE support services that were well-received during the previous project.
