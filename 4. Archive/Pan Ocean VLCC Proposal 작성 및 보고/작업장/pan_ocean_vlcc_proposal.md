# Focus and expertise in Tanker classification

## Track record of BV tankers

Our journey started in 1886 with the classification of the first tanker, "Glückauf," and has continued with the classification of some of the largest and most advanced tankers, including the ULCC "Bellamya" series.

### Current Fleet Status:
- **Active Tankers:** 1,423 ships
- **Order Book:** 136 units
- **Experience:** VLCC, Suezmax, Aframax, LR2, Panamax, LR1, MR, handy, and small tankers
- **Environmental Leadership:** 33 LNG-fueled tankers in service, 23 under construction
- **Innovation:** 16 methanol dual-fuel tankers under construction
- **Retrofit Solutions:** PREPARED notation for LNG, methanol, or ammonia retrofitting

## Global coverage for in-service surveys of tankers

BV has a worldwide network of 406 oil tanker qualified surveyors represented in 71 countries, ensuring efficient and swift response to all survey needs worldwide including major ports in Asia, Middle East, and globally.
