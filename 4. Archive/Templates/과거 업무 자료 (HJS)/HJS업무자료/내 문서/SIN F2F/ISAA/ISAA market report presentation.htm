TO:                    ISAA PRINCIPALS

FROM:              ROBERTO GIANNETTA – ISAA SECRETARY

 

 

In preparation for the upcoming ISAA Principals’ meeting in Singapore on 5 Dec, we hereby attach a copy of the market report presentation that the Secretary has compiled.


We would draw members’ attention to a couple anomalies that we were not able to immediately identify:

 

  1. Pakistan volumes in the past couple months seem a little off, compared to historic trends other years.  Typically we see demand dropping around July-Nov.  Yet, this year it seems that the demand has increased quite a bit.  Any suggestions?

 

  1. Exports from Japan seem to have dropped a bit.  This is particularly interesting to me as 2012 saw Japan’s exports affected by the tsunami and the aftermath.  Any suggestions?

 

Should members wish to offer any comments or suggested changes prior to our meeting, we are happy to tweak this presentation before we meet in Singapore.

 

 


Sincerely,


Roberto Giannetta
