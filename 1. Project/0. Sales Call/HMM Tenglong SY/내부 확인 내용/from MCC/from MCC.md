***Clarification on BBCHP under Korea Flag****

If the HMM plans to change the vessel’s flag to Korean Flag, please note that BBCHP scheme typically does not apply to the vessel directly registered under the Korean flag

in a legal and regulatory sense. Once the vessel is registered in Korea Flag, it must fully comply with the Korean Ship Safety Act regardless of its financing structure.
