**SINOKOR MARITIME CO.,LTD** agrees to class with **BUREAU VERITAS**

the ships owned and/or managed by them, subject to the latest provisions
of the Marine\
& Offshore Division General Conditions and Rules of this Classification
Society.\
(enclosed)

**BUREAU VERITAS** agrees to carry out classification and statutory
surveys on board vessels owned and/or managed by **SINOKOR MARITIME CO.,
LTD.** wth the purpose of issuing the relevant class and statutory
certificates at an agreed annual fee and with centralized invoicing.

**[ARTICLE 1]{.underline} - GENERAL**

1.1. Each of the surveys listed in Article 2, will be performed
completely and uninterruptedly

on occasion of the survey date scheduled, taking into account the
maximum possible

window.

1.2 In general terms, **SINOKOR MARITIME CO., LTD.** agrees to plan all
surveys

in close coordination with **BUREAU VERITAS**, to find the best cost
efficient solution for

**BUREAU VERITAS** carrying out the surveys.

1.3 Vessels may enter the agreement at any time, and once entered, shall
remain in the

agreement for a period of the classification term(Max. 5 years).

**[ARTICLE 2 -]{.underline}**

2.1 **The surveys covered by the agreement (as applicable) are:**

\- **CLASSIFICATION SURVEYS**

1.  Class renewal survey of hull, including additional surveys for those
    ships related to the

ESP Requirements.

2.  Class renewal survey of machinery, cargo, automated or refrigerating
    installation under normal,

Continuous, PMS survey system

3\. Annual surveys of hull, machinery, automation, boilers &
refrigerating installations, classed lifting appliances, inert gas,
cargo installations, installation of Liquefied Gas Carriers, Cargo
Installation of chemical of FLS Tankers, Centralized Navigation Control
installation

4\. Annual survey of structure

5\. Intermediate survey of Hull, ESP Requirements

6\. Classification Renewal Surveys for additional notations

7\. Tailshaft surveys.

8\. Bottom surveys (in dry-dock or in water).

9\. Internal, main or auxiliary boiler surveys.

\- **STATUTORY SURVEYS**

> Load-line surveys

1.  Load-line Harmonised periodical survey for the renewal of the
    load-line certificate.

2.  Load-line periodical inspection (annual).

SOLAS Surveys

3\. Periodical survey for the renewal of the harmonised safety
construction certificates.

4\. Mandatory safety construction annual and intermediate surveys.

5\. Periodical surveys for the renewal of the Harmonised safety
equipment certificates.

6\. Mandatory safety equipment annual and periodical surveys.

7\. Periodical servicing of launching appliances.

8\. Cargo ships safety harmonised Radio surveys, as required (radio
specialist's fees excluded).

\- **MARPOL**

**MARPOL Annexes**

1\. MARPOL Annex I Harmonised periodical survey.

2\. MARPOL Annex I annual and intermediate MARPOL surveys.

3\. MARPOL Annex II Noxious Liquids -- Periodical, Annual and
Intermediate surveys.

4\. MARPOL Annex IV -- Sewage -- Renewal survey.

5\. MARPOL Annex V -- Garbage -- Renewal survey.

6\. MARPOL Annex VI Air Pollution -- Renewal, Annual and Intermediate
survey.

7\. International Energy Efficient Annex VI issuance surveys.

8\. Ballast Water Management -- Renewal, Annual and Intermediate survey.

**IMO**

9\. Periodical surveys for the renewal of IMO Fitness gas certificate

10\. Mandatory annual and intermediate surveys for Gas and Chemical
Carriers.

\- **MISCELLANEOUS SURVEYS (AS APPLICABLE)**

1\. Quinquiennial & Quadrennial cargo gear renewal survey
(Derricks/Cranes).

2\. Annual cargo gear surveys (Derricks/Cranes).

3\. International Anti-Fouling System (IAFS) renewal survey.

2.2 **Surveys not covered by the agreement:**

This agreement does not include surveys in connection with:

\- Riding surveys

\- Damage surveys related to claims

\- Occasional surveys as a consequence of non compliance with article
1.1, performed outside

the range

\- Occasional surveys related to conversions

\- Occasional surveys for deletion of outstanding recommendations

\- Extension surveys

\- Lay-up/reactivation surveys

\- Occasional surveys related to modifications/alterations

\- Occasional surveys related to changes (name, managers, flag, port of
registry, etc.)

\- Occasional surveys further to PSC inspection

\- In general, any survey not covered by the Article 1 or not mentioned
in the Article 2.1

-   CAP survey; Article 2 of annex I to this agreement applied.

**[ARTICLE 3]{.underline} - PLACE OF SURVEYS**

-   Worldwide in stations staffed by exclusive surveyors, as listed in
    Marine Directory which is

> available at the website, www.veristar.com

**[ARTICLE 4]{.underline} - FEES**

4.1 Every five years period, depending on ship's age (1-10, over 10
years old), Survey fees

will be recalculated and the annual fee in the article 1 of annex I to
this agreement will be modified accordingly with owner's agreement

4.2 When new types of surveys introduced in the Rules of the Society are
added, the agreed annual

fee will be modified accordingly with owner's agreement.

4.3 Travel time during the normal working hours in order to carry out
surveys listed in article 2.1,

in a station listed in article 3 is included in the fixed annual fee.

4.4 **The annual fee do not include*:-***

\- Expenses for surveys carried out

\- Attendance outside normal working hours at owner's/Masters\' written
specific request.

\- Surveys under article 2.2

\- Travel time, when the survey is carried out outside survey station
listed in article 3

4.5 Surveys not covered by the agreement (listed in Article 2.2)
irrespective of the areas

performed, will be additionally and separately charged according to the
normal **BUREAU**

**VERITAS** fees scale in force at the time of the survey in the country
involved.

4.6 For Panamanian vessels an extra charge will be made to cover the
reimbursement to **BUREAU**

**VERITAS** of certification fees paid to the Panamanian Government. In
the cases of local taxes,

'

applicable to the national regulations of any survey centre are
independent, and will be charged

separately.(as applicable)

Any tax imposed by local legislation is charged separately.

**[ARTICLE 5]{.underline} - INVOICING AND SETTLEMENT OF FEES**

5.1 Invoices for Annual Fee will be presented by **BUREAU VERITAS**, at
the anniversary

Date of each ship's Class term and be paid by **SINOKOR MARITIME
CO.,LTD.**

within 30 days from issuance.

5.2 Invoices relative to occasional surveys described in paragraph 2.2
(survey not covered

by the agreement) will be presented by **BUREAU VERITAS**, with
necessary justification,

as soon as possible after the survey and be paid by **SINOKOR MARITIME
CO.,LTD.**

within 30 days from issuance.

5.3 Invoices relative to surveys carried out in stations not listed in
article 3 will be presented

by **BUREAU VERITAS**, with necessary justifications, as soon as
possible after the survey

and be paid by **SINOKOR MARITIME CO.,LTD** within 30 days from
issuance.

5.4 Further delay in settlement up to three months may be accepted by
Bureau Veritas at the

request of the Owner, subject to an added interest of 1.5% per month
after 3 month.

**[ARTICLE 6]{.underline} - DURATION AND TERMINATION**

1.  This contract is valid for 5-years from the date of signature.

2.  When the contract is terminated, Bureau Veritas scale of fees in
    force will be applied for all subject

> ships.

3.  A ship leaves the contract when her class is suspended, withdrawn,
    discontinued or

expired, or when the Owner does not comply with the conditions of the
article 5.

4.  This contract can be terminated by both parties further to a written
    notice not less than

three months.
