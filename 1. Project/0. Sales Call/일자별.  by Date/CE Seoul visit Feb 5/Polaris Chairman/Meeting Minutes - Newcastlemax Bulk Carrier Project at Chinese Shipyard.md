## 5. CHINESE SHIPBUILDING LANDSCAPE DISCUSSION

### 5.1 New & Reactivated Shipyards - Risk Analysis

**Market Trend:**
- Many new and reactivated yards emerging in China
- These yards are aggressively pricing to gain market share
- **Quality concerns:** Workforce availability and experience is a critical challenge

**BV's Warning on New/Reactivated Yards:**

**Historical Context (2010-2018 wave):**
- Many yards that shut down in 2010 and 2016-2018 are now being reactivated
- Previous generation (2010s) included many "village cooperative" style yards
  - Community-funded operations
  - Profit-focused rather than professionally managed
  - Not truly professional shipyards
  - Many failed during downturn

**Current Situation:**
- New reactivations may involve "more serious players" taking over facilities
- However, **main concern remains: qualified workforce availability**
- Market is extremely competitive for skilled labor
- Small yards cannot compete with salary levels of major groups like **Hengli (恒力集团)**
- New yards struggle to attract and retain qualified workers

**Building Model at New Yards:**
- Many new yards primarily build **blocks only**
- Blocks are then dispatched to main assembly yards
- These facilities may only dispatch 1-2 QMS (Quality Management System) personnel
- **Critical risk:** QMS staff may not be truly experienced

**BV's Strong Recommendation:**
- **"Stay with already proved shipyards"**
- Established yards like:
  - **DSIC (大连船舶重工 - Dalian Shipbuilding Industry Co.)** - mentioned for reference
  - **SWS (possibly 舜天威盛 or similar)** - where Polaris has existing experience
- These yards have:
  - Established track record
  - Ability to retain skilled workforce
  - Quality assurance systems
  - Financial stability
  
**Specific yards recently renamed:** BV mentioned some yards changed names recently and needed to find new names
