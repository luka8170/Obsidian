
# Logistics support for global energy projects

WR Logistics supports energy infrastructure projects with logistics services for conventional and renewable installations, focusing on safe handling and efficient execution.

## Logistics capabilities across conventional and renewable energy

WR Logistics provides logistics services for the energy sector, supporting delivery of oversized components, sensitive equipment, and time-critical cargo for power generation and distribution.

Key Features:

- Transport services for oversized power generation equipment
- Logistics for wind turbines and solar energy components
- Heavy-lift logistics for transformers and generators
- Planning and logistics for power plant construction
- International logistics support for energy infrastructure
