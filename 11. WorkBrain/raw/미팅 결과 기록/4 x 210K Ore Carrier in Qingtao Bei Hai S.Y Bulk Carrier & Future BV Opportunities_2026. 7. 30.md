[Meeting Minutes] Polaris Shipping – VLOC & VLEC Newbuilding Project Discussions 1. Project Schedule (Approximate) The steel cutting of the first vessel is scheduled to begin in 2030. The delivery of the first vessel is expected in 2031. 2. Key Discussion Points 2.1 Classification Structure and Cost Conditions - Dual Class and Single Class Construction stage (Dual Class): DNV will serve as the leading class, taking full responsibility for drawing approvals. BV will share statutory inspections and on-site surveys equally (50:50) with DNV during the newbuilding stage. Operation stage (Single Class): After delivery, the vessel will transition to BV as only class society. -Class maintenance costs and conditions BV's Cost proposal: Single class approx. USD 20,000 per year; dual class approx. USD 15,000 per year. Polaris's Requirement : Executive Director Jung requested that all temporary inspection expenses be included in the cost comparison for transparency. Moreover the final class fee structure must not exceed the level proposed by Korean Register (KR). 2.2 Request for Deployment of a Korean Surveyor On-Site As most surveyors at the construction shipyard are expected to be Chinese, smooth communication is essential. Polaris's Request: To ensure full compliance with Korean government regulations and the Korean Safety Act, at least one Korean surveyor with full knowledge of the rules must be stationed at the Chinese shipyard as a BV surveyor. BV internal review: BV will calculate the cost of dispatching one Korean surveyor to Qingdao BeiHai, factoring in approx. inflation since the project is scheduled to start in 2030. BV will reassess the financial feasibility of this deployment, considering the significant cost burden (including a 1.25x multiplier), in consultation with headquarters in France (Mustafa and the finance department). 2.3 Review of VLEC Cargo Tank Type and Technical Specifications - Project overview: Korea’s first VLEC project to transport U.S. ethane to Singapore, with Hanwha Ocean designated as the shipyard. - Cargo tank specification: GTT NO96 system under review. 2.4 Response to Government Special Regulations and Class Rule Coordination Polaris's Request for Korean government requirements: Executive Director Jung requested a list of requirements and regulations (including the latest Citadel updates). These will be shared with DNV, the leading class, to ensure compliance during drawing approval and to prevent technical issues when transitioning to BV single class later. 2.5 Joint Project with HMM and Technical Support - Joint technical support: The project will be jointly pursued by Polaris Shipping and HMM, based on the same vessel type and technical specifications. Polaris’s decisions (e.g., class designation) will have a significant influence on HMM’s choices. - BV cooperation plan: BV technical managers will attend future joint specification meetings with Hanwha Ocean and HMM to provide technical backup and strengthen BV’s commercial position.
