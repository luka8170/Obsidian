This Agreement for Joint Development Demonstration of Cybersecurity Solution (“Agreement”) enters into force on 04 Nov 2025 (“Effective Date”) by and between:

**Bureau Veritas SA Branch Office Korea** company registered under the laws of Republic of Korea whose registered address is 24th floor, 639 Jungang-daero, Busanjin-gu, Busan, KR (hereinafter referred to as “BV”);

And:

**HYUNDAI LNG SHIPPING** company registered under the laws of Republic of Korea whose registered address is 7th floor, 45 Namdaemun-ro 4-ga, Jung-gu, Seoul, KR (hereinafter referred to as “HLS”);

And:

**IGLOO Corporation** company registered under the laws of Republic of Korea whose registered address is 5~8th floor, 7 Jeongui-ro 8-gil, Songpa-gu, Seoul, KR (hereinafter referred to as “IGLOO”);

Each of COMPANY and BV are referred to herein individually as a “**Party**” and collectively as the “**Parties**”.

**WHEREAS** BV is a world-famous classification society. It operates in the Marine & Offshore industry offering classification, certification and related services.

**WHEREAS** HLS is one of Korea's leading shipping companies, providing a cybersecurity-enabled network environment (HLS Hyundai Princepia, LNG carrier) for this JDP.

**WHEREAS** IGLOO is a leading security company in Korea, providing security solutions and services. IGLOO has implemented a security information and event management (SIEM) solution for ships and will be verified by the Society.

**WHEREAS** the Parties wish to work with each other regarding Demonstration of Cybersecurity Solution on Hyundai LNG Shipping’s LNG Carrier (the “**Purpose**”)

governance

## Steering Committee

The Parties shall establish a team (the “**Steering Committee**”) comprised of one (1) representative of each of the Parties to supervise the implementation of this Agreement. The mandate of the Steering Committee shall last for the duration of the Agreement and shall consist of:

1. monitoring the progress of the work contemplated by this Agreement;
2. reviewing opportunities to bid for joint technical studies or the sale of the Service;
3. allocate appropriate resources and skills to opportunities pursued by the Parties under this Agreement;
4. co-ordinating and monitoring the progress of co-bids prepared in response to opportunities and the work of any project teams;
5. reviewing and amending, as appropriate, the scope of the Agreement ([Clause 3.1](#_Purpose_of_this)) and the Operating Model (appendix 1, section 2) to ensure market responsiveness and operational effectiveness in order to reach the Purpose of this Agreement ; and
6. generally attempting, in accordance with Clause 10, to resolve disputes relating to this Agreement.

The Steering Committee shall hold meetings on a regular basis and not less than every two (2) weeks at such places and times as may be mutually convenient. If all representatives agree, a meeting may be held by video conference or telephone conference call. In order to hold a meeting of the Steering Committee at least one (1) representative of each Party must be present. Steering Committee decisions shall be taken unanimously and always in the spirit of the Parties' cooperation.

## Project Manager Responsibilities

The Project Manager shall act as an intermediary between the Parties.

In particular, the Project Manager shall be responsible for:

1. Monitoring compliance by the Parties with their obligations;
2. Transmitting documents and information connected to the Project between the Parties as well as to any other concerned persons;
3. Managing the financial account of the JDP.

The Project Manager shall be present at all meetings of the Steering Committee and shall be responsible for:

1. Keeping minutes of each Steering Committee meeting.
2. Presenting the Parties' proposals for work plans and allocation of resources to the Steering Committee.
3. Coordinating the various activities of the Project.
4. Collecting reports from subcontractors and Working Groups.
5. Submitting reports according to the provisions set forth in Article 4.4.
6. Calling and running Working Group meetings if a Working Group is established.
7. The Project Manager shall not be entitled to act or to make any legally binding decisions on behalf of any Party.

ANNEX A1 – project technical specification

## Executive summary

This Joint Development Project involves Hyundai LNG Shipping (HLS), IGLOO Corporation, and Bureau Veritas M&O to verify that IGLOO’s SPIDER OT SIEM delivers services that meet IACS UR E26 requirements. It will lead to onboard verification of IGLOO's SPIDER OT SIEM solution installed on HLS's LNG carrier "Hyundai Princepia" when it arrives at Pyeongtaek port, Korea. This collaboration aims to validate cyber security systems in real maritime environments and establish certification standards for the industry.

## Operating model

The Parties shall use their best efforts in order to reach the Purpose of the Agreement.

### Project Objectives

Joint-objectives

Verify that SPIDER OT SIEM solution meets IACS UR E26 Cyber Resilient requirements through onboard testing in LNG carrier operations

BV’s objectives

- IACS UR E26 Compliance Verification:

Conduct onboard verification to assess whether the IGLOO SIEM solution (SPIDER OT) installed on the Hyundai Princepia vessel actually meets the IACS UR E26 Cyber Resilient requirements

- Cyber Resilience Certification Process Development:

Establish standardized verification procedures and guidelines for cyber security system certification of LNG carriers through actual vessel verification

Hyundai LNG Shipping’s objectives

- Enhancement of Fleet Cyber Security:

Obtain empirical data to improve cyber threat response capabilities and ensure operational continuity for the company's LNG carrier fleet.

- Regulatory Compliance and Competitive Advantage:

Achieve proactive compliance with international regulations and provide differentiated services to clients through the implementation of cyber security systems that satisfy IACS UR E26 requirements

IGLOO Corporation’s objectives

- Maritime-Specialized SIEM Solution Validation:

Demonstrate that SPIDER OT can effectively meet IACS UR E26 requirements in marine environments and establish product reliability.

- Market Expansion in Maritime Industry:

Strengthen market position in the global maritime cyber security sector and create additional business opportunities based on successful real-vessel implementation cases

## Scope of Work

Pre-Verification

- Review IGLOO’s documentation and IACS UR E26 requirements (BV)

- Prepare verification tools and procedures (IGLOO)

Onboard Verification

- Test SPIDER OT performance in maritime environment (IGLOO)

- Verify IACS UR E26 compliance requirements (BV)

- Assess cyber threat detection and response capabilities (BV & HLS)

- Evaluate system integration with vessel operations (BV & HLS)

Certification and Documentation

- Analyze verification results (BV)

- Issue compliance certification with comment if requirements are met (BV)
