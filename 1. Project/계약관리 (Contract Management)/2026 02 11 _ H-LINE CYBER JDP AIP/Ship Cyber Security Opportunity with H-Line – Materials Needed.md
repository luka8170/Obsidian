On the other hand, my team DA/CYBER is able to help shipowners comply with Resolution MSC(428)98 : implement a robust, effective and auditable cyber risk management on board vessels. This is basically ISM Code translated into cybersecurity.

In particular we help shipowners improving their documentation Cybersecurity Policy (or management plan) and Cybersecurity Procedures, knowing that Policy and Procedures are to be part of their Safety Management Systems and these documents can be audited by Flags during annual audits. We can send a surveyor on board during one day audit to randomly check if Policy and Procedures are correctly implemented.
