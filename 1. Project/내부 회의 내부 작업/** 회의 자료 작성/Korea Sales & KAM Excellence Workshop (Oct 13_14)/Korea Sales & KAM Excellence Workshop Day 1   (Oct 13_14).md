**주제****:** Korea | Sales & KAM Excellence Workshop Day 1

Dear All,

Here is a placeholder for our venue in October. Hopefully you will be freshly come back from your well deserved holiday break!

Let's begin the afternoon so you have some time to review your emails and catch up on your backlog during the morning. Do not hesitate to extend the invitation if necessary.

Key Objectives of the workshop are related to the continuous improvement of your local sales & account management skills & organization based on:

1. Training on M&O Sales & Account Management processes & best practices we have developed so you can implement strong and consistent routines / habits
2. Defining clear commercial priorities (to be formalized in the commercial plan) for the countries
3. Being comfortable in monitoring your own performance and improving predictability

**DAY 1 AGENDA – COMMERCIAL STRATEGY & ORGANIZATION**

**1:30PM -3:00AM**

Welcome, Round table & Introduction of “Sales & KAM Excellence” roadmap 

**3:00AM-4:30PM**

Review of your regional Activities through your commercial plan to define 2026 ambitions and key objectives.

Some elements to be discussed:

- Discussion on your local markets, key clients, key services, commercial strategy…etc
- Your sales & marketing organization, routine of meetings, local commercial capabilities / expertise, duality BVS
- Country sales objectives
- Specificities of your market or your organization we need to understand better to support you
- Business Development Practices – how do you engage locally with your existing clients and with new clients? How do you manage your existing accounts?
- Key Account management practices – Who are your Key accounts, how do you manage relationship with them?
- Collaboration with other BUs, head office, other OGs (if any)
- The current limitation you are facing to achieve your objectives

**4:30PM – 5:00 WRAP UP AND END OF THE DAY**

We would be glad to have a moment together for the dinner or a post workshop drink if it is convenient for you.
