

Dear Mr. Vaes,

I would like to report to you for a full update on the ongoing lashing claim issue involving Sinokor, along with a strategic proposal to strengthen our commercial position as we navigate this situation.

**1. Background**

Sinokor has been experiencing serious operational difficulties with 2,800 TEU vessels built at Hyundai Mipo Dockyard (HMD) with BV class. Due to the wider-than-standard hull design of these vessels, lashing forces under full load conditions exceed the permissible limits. As a result, despite their rated capacity of 2,800 TEU, the actual number of containers that can be safely loaded is only in the low 2,000s, causing significant cargo revenue loss for the client.

**2. Current Situation**

Sinokor holds HMD primarily responsible for this issue, given that HMD contracted directly with the lashing plan provider (GLS) and built the vessels accordingly. Sinokor is preparing to file a formal claim against HMD for approximately KRW 7 billion. BV is not a party to this claim and remains technically neutral.

That said, Sinokor has expressed dissatisfaction with BV as well, given that the problems arose despite loading being carried out in accordance with the Cargo Securing Manual (CSM) and lashing program approved by BV. This dissatisfaction, while separate from any legal liability, represents a relationship-level concern that must be addressed.

To mitigate the lashing force issue, BV has proposed the application of the RSSA (Reduced Ship Motions Acceleration) notation to the four vessels (HMD Hull Nos. 4225–4228). KPO presented Sinokor yesterday with a revised quote of EUR 26,931 — a reduction of approximately 23% from the original BV Paris estimate of EUR 35,010. However, Sinokor has pushed back, citing a lack of reference data on the expected improvement in lashing forces following RSSA application, and is strongly requesting that the service be provided at no charge.

**3. Key Risks**

Sinokor is currently advancing plans to order four 5,000 TEU newbuildings at a Chinese shipyard, with the shipyard selection process now underway. A broker of KR (Korean Register) background operating in China has been maintaining close contact with Sinokor's senior management and is actively lobbying for KR adoption during the shipyard selection process. In addition, a KR-affiliated technical advisor is stationed at Sinokor and is encouraging the selection of KR for newbuilding orders. Should BV respond passively or maintain its position on the RSSA fee of EUR 26,931, there is a very high risk of losing this newbuilding project to KR. Furthermore, this case could become a key reason for the technical advisor overseeing future newbuilding orders to steer Sinokor away from BV.

**4. Proposal**

To turn this situation into a strategic opportunity, I kindly request your review and approval to submit a request to HQ for a full waiver of the RSSA fee of **EUR 26,931 (covering four vessels, Routes 16, 17 & 18)**. While BV bears no legal liability in this matter, proactively absorbing this cost would serve as a strategic commercial investment — one that addresses the client's frustration and demonstrates BV's commitment to a genuine partnership.

**5. Action Plan**

Subject to your approval, the following actions will be taken:

- Submit a formal request to DA for the RSSA fee waiver
- Submit a formal request to DA for a technical seminar on "Container General & Lashing." BV Paris lashing expert Guillaume Brochet will be requested as the presenter. The seminar will be held in a hybrid format, with HQ (Paris) joining online and KPO (Busan) hosting an in-person session, to maximise attendance and engagement from Sinokor's management team

**6. Expected Outcome**

By combining the RSSA fee waiver with a technical seminar led by a leading BV expert, we can position BV as a proactive partner that voluntarily supports Sinokor through both its technical and financial challenges. This approach will help neutralise the influence of the KR broker and the KR-affiliated technical advisor stationed at Sinokor, and strengthen the grounds and confidence for Sinokor to select BV for its upcoming newbuilding orders.

I would appreciate your review and feedback before we proceed with submitting the waiver request to HQ.
