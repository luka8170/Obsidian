**Meeting report:** Pantar p**roject status review and discussion about action plan**

**Attendees**: Panstar site office representative (formerly, Panstar Tree CEO), KPO, NC, SJ HAN

**Date and Time:** 11 April, 2024 10:30 am in Panstar site office in Daesun S/Y

**Summary:** During the meeting, Mr. DH Han, Panstar site office representative, mentioned an in-depth overview of the current status of the project, highlighting important concerns related to drawings, approvals and project schedule. Key points discussed were as follows

1. **Drawing approval status:**

- Mr. DH Han expressed serious concerns about the approval status of critical drawings, particularly safety and fire-related designs and SRTP, noting that it is incomprehensible that many of these drawings are still pending approval despite being essential to the project's progress.

3. **Project schedule and delivery:**

- The urgency of the situation was underscored by the upcoming Osaka exhibition in April (April 13, 2025). DH Han emphasized that if the ship is not ready to be deployed before this event, the contract could be canceled, which could lead to a potential legal dispute between Daesun (DS) and Bureau Veritas (BV).

5. **Communication and coordination issues:**             **We** highlighted difficulties with communication between DS and BV engineers, including delayed responses and problems understanding comments and requirements. We noted that this lack of effective communication contributed to project delays and misunderstandings.
6. **Action plan:**

- DH Han emphasized a focused effort to resolve pending comments (BV's lack of comments on drawings submitted by DS, approximately 500) and expedite drawing approvals. There was a call for increased collaboration and accountability from both DS and BV to meet the requirements of the project.

8. **Immediate action:**

- To further address these issues, an internal meeting was scheduled between the head of the Drawing Approvals Department (DA) at the headquarters and the Korean team.
- We indicated that we would prepare a strong, formal message to BV headquarters to communicate the seriousness of the situation and urge swift action.

10. **Next deadline:**

- DH Han emphasized the need to make significant progress by the end of the month, especially in getting key drawings approved.
- The Chairman hoped to see significant progress by the end of March 2025 (the deadline for ship delivery that Panstar had notified the President), in line with the goal of having the ship in place before the Osaka exhibition.

12. **Potential consequences:**

- Failure to achieve the project milestones could result in the contract being canceled, with legal implications for both parties.

14. **Conclusion:**

- The meeting ended with a clear call to action and improved communication between DS and BV. 
- If immediate action is deemed necessary to resolve the pending issues and complete the project in a timely manner, a follow-up internal meeting to strategize further actions seems warranted. 
- While we fully sympathize with the frustration of the DS at BV headquarters with the sub-standard drawing submissions, we do not see a solution with this repetitive and circuitous response. 
- In particular, it should be noted that if this project is canceled or comes out with a lot of noise, the image of BV to other shipowners in Korea will likely be seriously damaged.
- In order for the project to proceed normally, it seems that BV should be exonerated of any wrongdoing, even if there is a legal case in the future.
