
MSC to order up to 10 VLCCs at Hengli? The more interesting question is: whois

really operating them.

  

Market talk (and recent reporting) suggests MSC is in serious discussions with Hengli

Heavy Industry for up to 10 VLCC newbuildings (often framed as 6 firm + 4 options),

with deliveries rumored for late 2028-2029.1

  

On the surface, this looks like MSC entering’ crude tankers, But I don’t think that’s

the cleanest read.

  

A more plausible structure: MSC (or an affiliated vehicle) acts as the capital provider

/ asset holder, while Sinokor-already in the middle of an unprecedented VLCC

build-up-acts as the operating platform, potentially via long-term charter

arrangements.

  

Why this interpretation matters:

Sinokor’s playbook is about control, not just ownership.

Beyond secondhand acquisitions, Sinokor has been aggressively locking in tonnage

via time charters at headline rates, The combined effect is a meaningful influence on

VLCC availability and market liquidity.

  

Hengli is already a VLCC builder with scale ambitions.

  

This wouldn’t be Hengli entering VLCC.” It would be incremental, high-profile

volume-and potentially a signal about how Chinese yards are positioning for the

next phase of the crude tanker cycle,

MSC’s strategic center of gravity remains elsewhere,

  

MSC has demonstrated a clear pattern in non-container segments: deploy capital to

secure assets and optionality, without necessarily building a full operating

organization in every market. In this case, owning VLcCs and chartering them out

could be a rational investment-style” move-especially it it helps unlock a broader,

more complex commercial arrangement with Sinokor (including Sinokors Container

fleet / container business options that have been circulating in the market).

  

If these VLCC newbuildings happen, the headline shouldn’t be”MSC becomes al

tanker operator.” The real story is likely a structured partnership-capital on one

side, operating scale on the other-reshaping the VLCC supply picture into 2028-

2029.
