## CALCULATION METHODOLOGY & ASSUMPTIONS

### A. Market Rate Assumptions

**1. Classification Annual Fee Market Rate: $8,000/year**
- Basis: Typical classification society annual fees for LNG carrier fleet
- Used for: Block fee savings calculation

**2. Commercial Training Rates:**
- Group training: $10,000 - $15,000/day (10-15 participants)
- Individual session: $2,000 - $4,000/session

**3. Professional Daily Rates:**
- HSSE Coordinator: $600 - $800/day
- HSSE Manager: $800 - $1,000/day
- QHSE Director: $1,500 - $2,500/day

### B. Time Period Assumptions

**Construction Period:**
- Duration: 24-30 months
- Used for: HSE support, training calculations

**Operational Period:**
- Duration: 15 years
- Used for: Block fee calculations

### C. Conservative Approach

1. **Service Valuation:** Lower end of market rates used for conservative estimates
2. **Time Estimation:** Conservative estimates of days/hours required
3. **Exclusions:** All standard classification services excluded
4. **Verification:** Only services explicitly mentioned as "free of charge" or "without extra cost" included

### D. Inflation

- Not adjusted for inflation over 15-year period
- All values in current USD (2024)
