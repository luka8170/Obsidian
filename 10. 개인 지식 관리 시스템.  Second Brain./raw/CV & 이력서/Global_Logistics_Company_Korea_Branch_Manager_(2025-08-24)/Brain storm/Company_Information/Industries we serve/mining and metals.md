

# Logistics for the mining and metals sector

WR Logistics supports the full mining lifecycle with logistics services for exploration, site construction, operations, and metals processing, including remote site and heavy equipment handling.

## Logistics support for mining and metals operations

WR Logistics provides logistics services for mining projects, from exploration to plant components and finished metals, with support for complex terrain and operational needs.

Key Features:

- Remote site logistics for exploration and active mines
- Transport and installation for heavy mining equipment
- Logistics for processing plant construction and equipment delivery
- Handling and distribution of finished metals
- Supply chain solutions for ongoing mine operations
