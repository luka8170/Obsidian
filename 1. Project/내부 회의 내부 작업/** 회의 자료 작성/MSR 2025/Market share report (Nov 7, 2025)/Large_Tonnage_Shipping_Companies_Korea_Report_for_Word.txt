EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report identifies and analyzes Korea's major large tonnage shipping companies, focusing on fleet size, vessel types, and classification society relationships. The analysis covers 844 Korean shipping companies operating 3,084 vessels totaling 72.6 million GT, plus significant orderbook positions, with particular emphasis on Bureau Veritas' current market position and growth opportunities.

Key Findings (Rankings based on Total Fleet = Operating + Orderbook):
- Top 10 Korean shipping groups (by total fleet including orderbook) control approximately 70% of Korean fleet capacity
- HMM leads with 12.5M GT (125 vessels total): 108 operating + 17 orderbook (15.7% growth)
- Sinokor Merchant #2 with 10.1M GT (190 vessels): 182 operating + 8 orderbook (4.4% growth)
- Pan Ocean #3 with 8.5M GT (125 vessels): 111 operating + 14 orderbook (12.6% growth)
- Cido Shipping #4 - CRITICAL OPPORTUNITY: 7.9M GT (125 vessels total): 72 operating + 53 orderbook (73.6% massive fleet expansion!)
- Bureau Veritas has strong presence in 6 of top 10 companies, particularly Sinokor Merchant (34 vessels)
- LNG carrier market represents BV's strongest position with 27.1% market share
- HMM Chinese Shipyard Opportunity: Active collaboration with BV China on 12 feeder container ships
- Total Korean Orderbook: 185 vessels representing significant classification revenue opportunities

Data Quality Note: Clarkson operating fleet data differs from actual BV contract portfolio. Example: 2022 BV secured 19 newbuilding vessels but Clarkson shows only 17. Orderbook data essential for accurate market assessment and ranking.
