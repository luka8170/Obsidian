**1. Why HMM was not in the June 8th report**

To explain why we did not report on HMM's newbuilding project, it is important to first clarify HMM's corporate structure and why they are effectively limited to KR as their classification society.

HMM is state-owned and controlled by Korea Development Bank (KDB). It is subject to annual parliamentary audits, and choosing a foreign class over KR requires formal justification — failure to do so can have serious consequences for HMM management. HMM's newbuildings are therefore structurally tied to KR, and we did not consider this a realistic BV opportunity.

**2. Our approach to HMM (long-term)**

We are building a BV track record with HMM through the Zhejiang Tenglong project (TL 2418 / TL 2420), currently under BV class. The latest sign is encouraging — HMM has indicated they will likely keep BV class rather than transfer to KR. Given their standard KR-only policy, this is a meaningful step forward.

Last month, we held a senior-level meeting with the CEO of HMM Ocean Service and the Head of Marine Management. As a follow-up, a joint technical visit with our FST team (Gerrit Bunt) is planned for July. Our long-term goal is to make HMM naturally see BV as a complement to KR — not an exception — leveraging BV's equal standing as a recognised organisation (RO).

**3. Vale Ore Carrier: focusing on Polaris Shipping**

Rather than HMM, we are concentrating our immediate efforts on Polaris Shipping for the Vale Ore Carrier project. We have confidential information from within Polaris that their Vale COA negotiations are nearly concluded. The expected order is 2 firm + 2 option (210K DWT Newcastlemax).

As you know, two days ago we met Chairman HS Han of Polaris in Seoul. He called himself a "Big Fan of BV" and committed his support for BV in their upcoming newbuilding program. Chairman Han has exclusively used BV for all of Polaris's newbuilding projects to date — including feeder container vessels at Huanghai Shipyard and 3 x Newcastlemax Bulk Carriers at SWS.

On top of this, Polaris's Busan technical team has been in close discussion with BV — and BV only — on the development of IACS rules for Ore Carriers. BV Korea will gather the necessary documentation from Polaris and share it with the relevant team at head office.
