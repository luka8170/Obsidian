Dear Alex,

We have been asked by Hyundai Glovis to submit a proposal for 1 x 174K LNGC to be built in HD Hyundai Samho for which ITOCHU is charterer.

It is informed that KR is considered as well and is offering very low fees while in service.

In this view, we have run studies with several scenarios considering various fees and in-service surveys done in and out Korea.

Below are the calculations retained which considered the same approach done for K-Line+Glovis for the QE Batch 2 (but without providing HSE supervisor that would add around USD 130K for the duration of the construction and reduce further the profitability informed below).

- It is based on one(1) off 174K LNG carriers which is supposed to be built in HD Hyundai Samho Co., Ltd. based on recent shipbuilding contact between shipyard and owner.
- It is based on single classification society as BV including classification and statutory scope of work and also design review and survey according to BBCHP scheme.
- For NS, we assumed as below.

·        Navigation scope does not include activities in Korea against prospect charter

·        Block fee for each term is same as previous two(2) off Hyundai Glovis LNG carriers in Hanwha Ocean for QatarEnergy project

1st term : Free

2nd term : 4,000 USD per year

3rd term : 8,000 USD per year

![](cid:image013.png@01DC6EDC.B50CFCE0)

- Overall profitability estimation includes NC, BVN and NS shows 1.5% for whole life of the vessel as shown on screen shot below with details of revenue and cost.

![](cid:image014.png@01DC6EDC.B50CFCE0)

Sang-Jin mentioned some intelligence in Hyundai Glovis that they may proceed with additional 1+1 174K order in Hanwha next year bit there is no charter defined at this moment. However, Sung Yong has just checked with Hanwha Ocean and it seems that there is no discussion for LOI or slot reservation with Hyundai Glovis so far.

Should these additional 1+1 LNGC in Hanwha come true, Glovis will require for sure same conditions…

Based on the above, I would not proceed with this prospect unless we consider it as strategic.
