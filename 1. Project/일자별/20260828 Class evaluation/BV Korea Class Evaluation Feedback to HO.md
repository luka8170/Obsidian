## Scope of this input

The KOSA survey is a **shipyard** evaluation — it reflects DA/plan-approval and surveyor performance as seen by the yards. The overall Design & Rule score and ranking is better commented on by DA/QHSE, who own that data directly. As the owner-relations contact, I don't think it's my place to interpret the yard-side scorecard.

What I can add is direct, first-hand context on the **two specific incidents Christophe already flagged** — the PanStar ferry and the CTV project — since both involve Korean owners I manage. These are live relationship issues, not just data points in a survey.

## 1. PanStar Miracle (SB682, Daesun S/Y, Ro-Pax ferry, IMO 43487V)

- **Apr–May 2024**: drawing approval backlog escalated badly — 265 drawings total, only 227 reviewed with **1,930 comments issued** (avg 8+/drawing, flagged internally as "highly unusual"), SRTP concept resubmitted 4 times.
- **Owner escalation**: PanStar's President (Jae Geun Kwon) sent a direct warning to BV HQ and Busan office (23–28 May 2024) referencing the risk of the shipyard contract being cancelled and stating the owner "may consider action against both parties." This was PanStar's **first newbuilding project with BV**, so there was no track record to fall back on.
- **BV response**: Korea CE personally intervened, a DA Paris PM (Roberto Spolaore) flew to Korea, and BV logged 3,366 man-hours on hull structure alone — a 240% overrun.
- **Outcome**: the vessel was delivered on schedule and made her maiden voyage Busan–Osaka on 13-Apr-2025, in time for the Osaka Expo deadline the owner had been pushing hard on. So it ended well operationally — but only after a CEO-level escalation.
- **Owner-relations read**: trust took a real hit before it recovered. PanStar is an active account (currently under FYF contract discussion), so I'd treat this as a relationship that needs continued proactive reinforcement, not a closed file.

## 2. CTV — designer/BV technical dispute (K-WIND Solution, Hull 49038E, 27m CTV, Eunsung H.I.)

- The owner's appointed designer (Junghae Engineering) submitted an FEM analysis that BV's own calculation disagreed with, on required local reinforcement. BV Singapore (SPO) reviewed it independently and required additional reinforcement work.
- This is a direct match for what Christophe described — the owner's chosen designer's numbers didn't hold up under BV's independent check, and the resulting rework is what's driving the delay.
- **Impact on delivery**: slipped from 31-Mar → 31-Jul → 31-Aug → ~end-Sept 2025. Owner (K-Wind Solution, Jerald Ahn) sent a formal "[URGENT] Request for Expedited Resolution" in Aug 2025.
- **Structural issue for us**: the technical call sits with BV Singapore SPO; BV Korea (KPO) is in the middle, facilitating between the owner/designer/yard and Singapore, without authority to resolve the disagreement locally. The owner experiences this as "BV Korea can't get us an answer," even though the delay isn't within Korea's control.
- **Worth flagging as a pattern, not a one-off**: a second Korean CTV owner (Yeosu Ocean / Ventus Marine, Camellia 47376X) raised a very similar complaint in parallel — IP Code certification stuck 2+ months at BV Singapore, "field surveyors and plan approval office disconnect," and the chairman told us directly he saw the relationship at risk. Two independent CTV owners hitting the same Korea↔Singapore SPO friction in the same period suggests this is a segment-level gap, not owner-specific noise — relevant given CTV/offshore wind is a segment we're actively targeting for 2027 growth.

## 3. What I'd ask from HO — specific to these two cases

- **CTV/offshore wind segment**: a clearer, faster escalation channel between Korea and Singapore SPO for Korean-owned CTV projects, so Korea isn't absorbing owner frustration for delays it has no authority to resolve. This is worth fixing proactively given it's a growth segment for us.
- **Large NB projects with heavy DA Paris involvement**: loop in Korea BD/relationship contacts earlier when a comment backlog is visibly building, so escalation doesn't have to reach owner-CEO-to-BV-HQ level before HQ engages, as happened with PanStar.
- **Relationship follow-through, not just process fix**: both PanStar and K-Wind Solution had real trust damage. I'd like to keep proactively engaging both accounts (beyond just closing the technical items) — PanStar has more newbuilding/FYF potential, and K-Wind Solution is active in a segment we want to grow in.
