# BV Track records of LNG carriers

BV is a leading class society, founding member of the International Association of Classification Societies and is deeply involved in the LNG distribution chain including the classification of LNG carriers since 1965 with the 25,000 m3 **_JULES VERNE_**.

### BV has a proven record and long experience in the classification of LNG carriers. We believe that LNG has a role to play as a transition fuel on the route to zero carbon.

To-date, **246** LNG carriers have been classed by BV out of which **128** are units currently in service (including 8 LNG bunkering ships built in The Republic of Korea, China and Europe), **3** are under conversion and **69** are on order (including 4 LNG bunkering ships at several yards in the Republic of Korea, Singapore, China and Spain). Our reference list of LNG carriers also includes **16** ships classed after construction with BV (transfer of class from another class society), and a few other ships transferred to other class societies or recycled.

With reference to LNG containment systems BV has been engaged in the examination of multiple systems and has given its approval to the major ones including the GTT membrane systems (mainly NO and Mark) and the independent type B systems such as Moss and IHI SPB. In particular the latest technology NO96 Super+ has been assessed and is to be installed for the first time ever in 4 LNG carriers to be built in HANWHA OCEAN. We are involved in the classification of the largest LNG carriers with both GTT and Moss type containment systems with 4 cargo tanks, respectively SK AUDACE (180,000 m3 GTT Mark III Flex), LNG SCHNEEWEISSCHEN (180,000 m3 GTT NO96) and PACIFIC BREEZE (182,000 m3 Moss), and in the

classification of the largest FSRU in service the BAUHINIA SPIRIT (Ex-MOL FSRU CHALLENGER 263,000 m3 with 5 GTT NO96 tanks). Furthermore BV has been the first class involved in LNG carrier classification of a 200,000 m3 capacity with 4 tanks (fourteen units, three of them in service are included in the statistics of this document). More information related to LNG carriers by cargo containment system in the range of 170,000 to 200,000 m3 cargo capacity is detailed in paragraph 2.3.1.

## Experiences with alternative technologies

## Air Lubrication System (ALS)

As mentioned in the previous paragraph in addition to new reliquefaction and subcooling systems, the most common technologies usually proposed in LNG carriers are the shaft generators (PTO) and air lubrication systems (ALS).

57 modern 174,000 ~ 180,000 m3 LNG carriers in service are equipped with PTO technology and there are 98 on order some of them with BV class. This may be an obvious proven technology to save fuel during navigation considering the SFOC of the 2-stroke DF engines compared with the DF generator sets. Clutch solutions can be proposed and potentially power take in (PTI) and hybrid propulsion for peak saving and/or low speed and ship manouvring or use of alternative power units rather than internal combustion engines to propell the LNG carriers.

ALS systems have been implemented also very recently in the LNG carrier fleet. There are currently 44 modern 174,000 ~ 180,000 m3 LNG carriers in service and 133 on order equipped with any of the available systems, basically HANWHA OCEAN, HHI-Himsen, SHI-Saver air or Silverstream. From the operation point of view the sea condition is an important parameter to be considered.

As mentioned above, in combinantion PTO and ALS may provide fuel savings in the range of 4-5% during navigation considering in the equation the energy needed to produce the compressed air for the ALS. BV can develop specific studies with regards to this equipment.

In addition to the above mentioned techologies, BV is currently involved in projects with the following new technologies among others.

## VeriSTAR Green

#### VeriSTAR Green is a convenient, cloud-based application designed to help shipowners stay up-to-date and comply with the IMO (EEDI, CII, DCS), EU-ETS, EU-MRV and UK-MRV regulations.

IMO Regulations through the DCS (Data Collection System) requirements set in MARPOL Annex VI require ships over 5,000GT to collect and report their fuel consumption, associated CO2 emissions and CII (Carbon Intensity Indicator). Discussions are currently on-going to detail the upcoming requirements for GHG emissions of shipping industry and possible associated cost, respectively the IMO LCA Guidelines and the Market-Based Measures.

EU and UK Regulations similarly require fuel consumption reporting via EU & UK MRV (Monitoring, Reporting & Verifying).

In addition, EU also extends the reporting of others GHG than CO2, namely CH4 and N2O, as of 2024 as well as includes the ETS mechanism to the maritime transport, forcing shipping companies to cover their EU ETS exposure with the corresponding amount of EUAs (EU Allowances).

Finally, FuelEU Maritime will apply as of 2025 and will include other metrics like GHG intensity of fuels and onshore power supply for container and passenger ships.

# Classification notations

In this section we refer to proposed and optional notations described in the **Bureau Veritas NR467 Rules for the classification of steel ships**. Other rule or information notes (NR or NI) mentioned below describe the requirements for some specific notations.

## Proposed class notations

The following class notations are proposed.

#### I HULL MACH Liquefied gas carrier (LNG, Ship type 2G, 0.35 barg, -163⁰C), IATP, Unrestricted navigation, VeriSTAR-Hull CM, Spectral Fatigue (Worldwide, 40 years), AUT-UMS, SYS-NEQ-1, ERS- S, LNGfuel dual fuel, INWATERSURVEY, LI-HG-S3, BWT, GREEN PASSPORT, ESA, CPS(WBT), AVM-DPS,

**MON-SHAFT, CLEANSHIP, BOG-reliquefaction**

The description of each class notation is the following:

**I:** (Class Symbol) is assigned to ships built in accordance with the requirements of the Bureau Veritas Rules and maintained in a condition considered satisfactory by the Society.

**HULL:** special survey at yard for hull construction by Bureau Veritas.

**MACH:** special survey at yard for machinery installations by Bureau Veritas.

**Liquefied gas carrier (LNG, IMO Type 2G, 0.35 barg, -163 °C)**: service notation LNG carrier with further description about the type of product and the conditions for transportation. Maximum Cargo Density 500 kg/m3 could be added.

**Unrestricted navigation:** operation in any area and any period of the year.

**VeriSTAR-Hull CM:** ship the structural condition of which is checked with 3D FEM calculation program at design stage according to the requirements of the Society. A fatigue assessment has been carried out on selected structural details showing that their evaluated design fatigue life is not less than 40 years for North Atlantic trade.

**Spectral Fatigue (Worldwide, 40 years):** the notation may be assigned when the fatigue check of structural details is performed through spectral fatigue techniques. The information between brackets is a short description of routes and areas considered for this spectral fatigue analysis and associated design fatigue life. The Guidance Note NI611, Guidelines for Fatigue Assessment of Steel Ships and Offshore units, is to be used.

**AUT-UMS:** ship fitted with automated installations enabling machinery spaces to remain periodically unattended in all sailing conditions including manoeuvring.

**SYS-NEQ-1:** ship fitted with a centralized navigation control system so laid out and arranged that it enables normal navigation and manoeuvring operation of the ship by one person, for periodical one man on watch.

**ERS-S:** damage longitudinal strength and damage stability analyses performed in an emergency situation (accident). It aims at providing information on the remaining hull strength and stability after the accident. See NR556.

**LNG fuel dualfuel:** dual fuel installation in accordance with IGC Code chapter 16 and relevant BV classification rules (NR467 Part D Chapter 9).

**INWATERSURVEY:** may be assigned to ships provided with suitable arrangements to facilitate the in-water surveys.

**LI-HG-S3**: ship equipped with a loading instrument performing; hull girder calculations and intact stability calculations and direct damage stability calculations based on pre-programmed damage cases.

**BWT:** ship fitted with a ballast water treatment plant. See NI538.

**GREEN PASSPORT:** ship for which requirements intended to facilitate ship recycling have been applied, encompassing the identification, quantification and localization of materials which may cause harm to the environment and people when the fittings or equipment containing such materials are removed, or when the ship is recycled. See NR528. GREEN PASSPORT EU to be considered for ships with EU flag.

**ESA:** compliance of the ship propulsion shaft lines with NR592 “Elastic Shaft Alignment”.

**CPS (WBT):** ship complying with the requirements of Coating Performance Standard (PSPC). See NR530

**AVM-DPS:** The additional class notation AVM-DPS is assigned to ships which are fitted with a duplicated propulsion system enabling them to maintain operating conditions with some limitations in power (but 50% of the main power is to be maintained), speed, range and comfort, in the case of single failure of items relative to the propulsion or power generating system.

**MON-SHAFT:** ship fitted with a temperature monitoring system for the tailshaft stern tube aft bearing. The assignment of this notation allows the ship to be granted a reduced scope for complete tailshaft surveys.

**CLEANSHIP:** ship so designed and equipped as to control and limit the emission of polluting substances in the sea and the air.

**BOG-reliquefaction**: The ship is equipped with system(s) to re-liquefy the BOG produced in the cargo tanks.

## Optional class notations

BV can provide several optional notations. Some interesting notations in the frame of this project which would enhance the sustainable footprint of the project and improve the crew well-being are described as follows:

**WPS**: Ships fitted with a wind propulsion system may be assigned the following additional class notations:

- **WPS1** for wind propulsion system including standing part.
- **WPS2** for wind propulsion system including standing and running part.

BV regulation NR206 Wind propulsion systems describe the specific requirements.

**FuelCell**: this additional service feature can be granted to Ships fitted with a fuel cell system and complying with the requirements of BV NR547. With respect to the primary fuel considered, assumed it is LNG, the following additional service feature would be mandatory: LNGfuel

**OCC**: Additional service feature assigned to ships fitted with a carbon capture and storage (CCS) system intended to separate the carbon dioxide (CO2) from the ship engine exhaust gases and store it on board.

**OCC-PREPARED**: This additional class notation may be assigned to new ships designed with specific arrangements intended to accommodate an onboard carbon capture and storage system at a later stage. It may also be assigned to existing ships when the installation of a CCS system is considered during a future retrofit.

**COMF-NOISE 3**: Comfort with regard to noise criteria applicable to specified ship category 3. **COMF-VIB 3**: ship satisfying levels of vibration defined in BV rules. The assessment of vibration is carried out through measurements during harbour and sea trials.

**CYBER RESILIENT:** is assigned to ship in order to reflect that the ship complies with the requirements regarding the following key elements to reinforce ships resilience when dealing with cyber-attacks: identify the computer-based systems on board, protect, detect anomalous activity, respond to attack, and recover the systems and restore.

All the new building vessels after the shipbuilding contract signature in January 2024 shall be built under IACS UR E26, which is equivalent to this notation.

**IATP** : The additional class notation IATP (increased admissible cargo tank pressure) may be assigned to ships intended to carry methane (LNG) whose maximum cargo tank design pressure does not exceed 70 kPa and that are designed and built so as to allow the pressure in the tanks to increase above 25 kPa.

**ALP-MR** : The additional class notations ALP may be assigned by the Society in lieu of the notations when the corresponding lifting appliances meet the requirements of specific National Regulations under the conditions defined in NR526 and may be completed by -MR when in addition, lifting appliance are intended to be used for lifting of personnel

In certain instances, authorities may impose a maximum cargo capacity requirement for accessing certain LNG terminals.
