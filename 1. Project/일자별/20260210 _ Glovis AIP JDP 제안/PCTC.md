Dear Mr. Han,

If we look at the recent PCTC industry development projects, they are concentrated on the decarbonization and alternative fuels. As example, the recent Grimaldi Group deliveries included several ammonia-ready car carriers.  There is an expectation of higher capacity vessels in the next generation of PCTCs, and which will also lead to advanced hull designs in addition to the focus on propulsion improvements / lower fuel consumption. Like other segments, the operational technologies are becoming part of modern PCTC projects with AI-powered navigational safety and fleet analytics systems (ex. Gram Car Carriers are adopting Orca AI). The market is also seeing logistical strategy shifts as some automotive manufacturers (ex. BYD)  are investing in their own car-carrier fleets to control logistics etc..

Installation and use of WAP systems are also being considered. Integrating hull support structure in a light superstructure with thin plates, may challenge the structure engineering of these units.

Needless to say, the Fire-fighting / hazards with EVs onboard, features to avoid overload and charging of EVs, etc., is probably the most common subject these days.
