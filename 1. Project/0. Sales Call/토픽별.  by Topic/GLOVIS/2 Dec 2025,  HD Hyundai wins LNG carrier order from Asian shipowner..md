
# HD Hyundai wins LNG carrier order from Asian shipowner

Shipbuilder has achieved 91.5% of its annual target

HD Hyundai Group has confirmed it has won an order to build an LNG carrier, beefing up its massive orderbook.

In a regulatory filing on Tuesday, HD Korea Shipbuilding & Offshore Engineering (HD KSOE) said the deal is with “a shipping company based in Asia”.

The KRW 367.2bn ($250m) contract contributes 5.24% to the South Korean shipbuilding group’s overall sales.

The ship will be constructed by Mokpo-based HD Hyundai Samho for delivery by 31 December 2028.

Sources indicated that the newbuilding is for [Hyundai Glovis](https://www.tradewindsnews.com/gas/hyundai-glovis-confirms-400m-charter-deal-to-back-single-lng-newbuilding/2-1-1907460), which last week announced it will build a 174,000-cbm LNG carrier after winning a 15-year time charter worth around KRW 580bn.

Japanese trader Itochu has previously been named by TradeWinds as the charterer.

This latest order will boost HD Samho’s revenue for 2025 to KRW 7trn ($4.77bn).

HD Hyundai Group is the parent holding company of all Hyundai subsidiaries, and HD KSOE is its shipbuilding intermediary.

HD KSOE said it had now secured $16.52bn in orders this year for 118 newbuildings, achieving 91.5% of its annual target of $18.05bn.

It has won contracts for nine LNG carriers, six LNG bunker vessels, nine LPG/ammonia carriers, two ethane carriers, 71 container ships, 18 tankers and three product carriers.
